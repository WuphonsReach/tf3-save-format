# Line ids, names, vehicles and the load set

How to get from a line's entity id to its name, its vehicles and the cargo its stops load, and where that stops working. Companion to [lines.md](lines.md) (stop times, trip plans). Labels as in [README.md](README.md#evidence-labels).

## In short

This is a log by nature: one catalog save (6425796) plus one Tiny game on 604, and one Giga game on 605 checked with 26 lines a mod's log named. The conclusions (the first was checked on 2026-11-08, the rest earlier):

- **A line's or vehicle's id gives its name through the [entity table](entity-table.md) (Observed, 601 to 605, twelve games).** The entity's entry holds a `(name type, slot)` pair, and the slot is the name's row in the entity names table. Every known pair matched: 26 of 26 lines named by a mod's log (605, two saves of one game), 14 of 14 lines and 55 of 55 vehicles named by notifications (604, catalog 6425796), and every warned line and vehicle whose id still held one in ten more catalog saves (601 to 605; four vehicle ids in [6446370](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/knowledge-2) had been reused by entities of two to four types). Lines are the entities with exactly six types, among them type 10, the name type N, N + 1 and N + 2 (Observed, 601 to 605, 13 saves), so the table lists every line with its name, warning or not ([below](#the-entity-table-names-every-line-604-and-605)). The line's own record (the array of that type) was not followed.
- **Outside the save, a game script can name them too.** A mod that prints a line's id with its name, or the Transit Overlay export ([transit-overlay.md](transit-overlay.md)), gives the pairs. Without one, a line can be matched to its row in the Lines window by carrier, cargo, vehicle count and Frequency ([lines.md](lines.md#the-lines-windows-frequency-rate-and-load-columns-604)) measured from a per-delivery log (605, Observed: 15 of 15 matches right, one guess between three look-alike lines wrong).
- **Cargo-unit records link a line to its vehicles** (line id, vehicle id, 0, origin industry id, a clock value; the third word was 1 in the 605 game), but only while the vehicles carry cargo. A line's id also sits in each of its vehicles' records, which works before any cargo is loaded (604, one Tiny game; the record's shape was not found in the 605 Giga game).
- **Name slot order does not give the link;** a line and its vehicles share a run only when made together. The slot is stored, in the entity table.
- **The stop record ends in a 37-byte vector** with one byte per cargo-list entry; 1 means the stop loads it.
- **Open.** What a cargo-unit record counts, which entity a stop record belongs to, whether a lower load percentage changes the vector, and which of a line's other entity-table types holds its stops.

## What was found

**Observed** on one save, catalog [6425796](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/mynewsavenotfinished1) (temperate, 1937, 735 MB stream), against the game's line window for the line Wool1 (four trucks, Road Vehicle 255 to 258; stop 1 loads wool, stop 2 unloads). Nothing here was tried on a second game.

- **In the notifications, a line's id and name sit together only in `line_warning` entries.** Each entry's `entity` is the line's entity id and `simParams.lineName` its name ([notifications.md](notifications.md#params-by-type)). The save had 14 such entries for 14 lines; Wool1 is 305,849. A vehicle's id and name sit together in the `simParams.mapping` of `cargo_rtc_warning` and `vehiclecondition` entries: Road Vehicle 255, 256 and 257 are 301,996, 303,875 and 305,653. A line or vehicle with no warning cannot be named this way, but the [entity table](#the-entity-table-names-every-line-604-and-605) names it. The line's id occurs nowhere else as a `u32`, `u64` or `f64` except in the records below, so the line's own record is not found, and so is not its list of stops.
- **Cargo units name their line and vehicle.** Records of five `u32` in a row, in a stretch with thousands of them: line id, vehicle id, 0, the id of the cargo's origin industry, which repeats for one line (237,445 for Wool1, Westbury Cotton Farm #1; see [statistics-lists.md](statistics-lists.md#groups)), and a clock value below the save's clock. Search for a known line id, then read the vehicle id behind it. For Wool1 this gave 25 records and four vehicles: the three named above and 303,923, which is the fourth truck by elimination (**Open**: its name was not read). Seven words (28 bytes) before the line id sits the cargo id (25, wool); the line "Wool Zachod 1" (284,733, five vehicles) also reads 25. A second shape has floats where the cargo id would be and an earlier line and vehicle before it; the line 'Wool, Wood, Gas, Meat, Plastic 1' (305,544) appears in it with Train 57 and Train 61 (ids 305,545 and 225,971 from the mapping). What a record counts (a cargo unit in transit, a trip leg) is **Open**. A vehicle with nothing aboard has none, so this gives a line's vehicles only while they carry cargo. Train 38 (line Clothes Train2, empty, no warning) could not be tied to its line this way.
- **A line's id sits in each of its vehicles' records (604, Observed, one Tiny subarctic game, four buses).** With the id and name taken from the Transit Overlay's log export ([transit-overlay.md](transit-overlay.md)), the id was not found anywhere in a save with the line and no vehicle, and was found four times, one per bus, in a save with four. This is a second route from a vehicle to its line, that works before the vehicle carries any cargo. What the fields around it hold is Open.
- **Name slots do not give the link.** In the entity names table Wool1 sat in slot 19,065, the line Zachos Gas 1 in 19,066 and Road Vehicle 255 to 258 in 19,067 to 19,070. Clothes Train2 sat in slot 2,754 and Train 38 in 12,688. So a line and its vehicles share a run of slots only when they were made together. Over 40 vehicles named in notifications, ids did not follow slot order, and none of four slots was found next to its entity id.
- **The stop record ends with a per-cargo byte vector (Observed, 547 records).** The last field of the [stop configuration record](lines.md#the-stop-configuration-record) is `u32 37` and 37 bytes, one per entry of the save's cargo list (37 there, [cargo-ids.md](cargo-ids.md)); a byte of 1 means the stop loads that cargo. 412 records held only index 0 (passengers), 46 were empty (stops that only unload) and the rest held freight. The three stops of the line 'Wool, Wood, Gas, Meat, Plastic 1' held {25}, {7, 12, 20, 29} and {25}: wool, then meat, logs, fuel and plastic, the five cargos in its name. The records of one line follow each other. The window's 100% is the only value seen, so whether a lower percentage changes the byte is **Open**. Four records of the 60-second kind (train, tram or ship) had no vector and a shorter shape, and no 60-second record held clothes (27), so Clothes Train2's loading stop was not found (**Open**; the three 30-second ones holding 27 belong to road lines). Which entity a stop record belongs to is also **Open**: the id after the three floats of the record that matched the wool loading stop named in a cargo record (237,457) held passengers only, so that id is not simply the record's own stop.

## 605: 26 lines named by a mod's log

**Observed**, 605, one save of the [Giga temperate game](test-games.md#giga-temperate-game-wr26a) (game date 1974-06-18, 54 lines, 19,390 names). The [Best Before mod](https://github.com/WuphonsReach/tf3-best-before-mod) (`best_before_1`, version 1.2-dev) printed one `lineName` entry per line it tracks, with the line's entity id and name, after the save was loaded again; its earlier version printed per delivery the line id, the vehicle id (`lastVehicle`), the cargo id, the carrier and the game time.

- **Matching by the Lines window was right where it was sure.** Before the names were printed, the delivery log was matched to two screenshots of the Lines window by carrier, cargo and vehicle count, then by Frequency: one vehicle's median time between unloads, divided by the line's vehicles, came within 1 % of the window on most lines. The largest number of units one vehicle unloaded at one stop separated two fuel lines of four trains each (100 and 80, the window's capacity over vehicles). All 15 matches called sure or likely agreed with the names; the guess among three log lines with two trains of 64 each and Frequencies 2 % apart was wrong (off by 4 % on the right line).
- **No search near the names tied the id to the name** (the [entity table](#the-entity-table-names-every-line-604-and-605) does). Each of the 26 names was in the [entity names table](entity-names.md) once. Id minus slot ran from 165,432 to 407,690. No occurrence of an id (4 to 272 per line) had its slot as a `u32` within 512 bytes. No base and stride from 4 to 64 bytes put more than 2 of the 26 ids at their slot's place in a list (chance), and no table indexed by id at a stride of 4 to 16 held the slot or slot + 1. The three lines in slots 12,106 to 12,108 never sat next to each other as ids. The mod's log of the same game from 1974 to 2032 kept these 26 ids, each with one name throughout, so the run's end save (`2032-test 1025-1342`, [test-games.md](test-games.md#giga-temperate-game-wr26a)) is a second save for this search (the names come from the game at run time, not from the save). These searches failed because the id is stored nowhere: it is the entry's position in the entity table.
- **No `line_warning` notifications** were in this save, so the route of the first section gave nothing.

## The entity table names every line (604 and 605)

**Observed**, 2026-11-08, read with `tools/town_places.py`'s table reader and a scratch script.

- **605, the Giga game's saves `1974-test 1025-1103` and `2032-test 1025-1342`.** Each of the 26 ids the mod's log named had the entry `{10, 16, 17, 18, 72, 75}` (type numbers of that save; 16 is the name type), and its name slot held the name the log gave: 26 of 26 in both saves. Type 75 was on 54 entities, the game's 54 lines, with slots 0 to 53; type 17 was on 55, the other types on hundreds or thousands. The 28 lines the mod does not track were the other 28 entities of that set.
- **604, catalog [6425796](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/mynewsavenotfinished1).** The lines' entry was `{10, 22, 23, 24, 67, 72}` (22 is the name type there), and type 72 was on 126 entities. The 14 `line_warning` pairs (`tools/notifications.py --type line_warning`) and the three line ids of the first section (Wool1, Wool Zachod 1, 'Wool, Wood, Gas, Meat, Plastic 1') all matched, and so did the 55 vehicle id and name pairs of its `vehiclecondition` and `cargo_rtc_warning` entries (trams, trains, road vehicles; their entries carried 8 to 10 types). Clothes Train2, the line that had no warning and no cargo, is id 260,471; its id occurs 18 times as a `u32` outside the table, which were not read for its train.
- **The rule that lists the lines (601 to 605, 13 saves).** Take the name type N from the names table's header (type 16, 19 or 22 in these saves), and keep the entities with exactly six types, among them 10, N, N + 1 and N + 2. In each save they shared one type set, whose sixth type is carried by no other entity. It gave 54 lines in the Giga game and 126 in 6425796, the anchored counts above. In the ten catalog saves with the most vehicle units, and in [6459692](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/harsewinkel), it gave 30 to 363 lines, and 44 of the 45 `line_warning` entities were among them with the warning's name. The 45th ([3333](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/3333), id 51,759) had a ten-type entry with no name pair: its id was reused after the warning. The company's entry is `{10, N - 1, N, N + 1, N + 2}`, so N + 1 and N + 2 are on the company too.
