# Line ids, names, vehicles and the load set

How to get from a line's entity id to its name, its vehicles and the cargo its stops load, and where that stops working. Companion to [lines.md](lines.md) (stop times, trip plans). Labels as in [README.md](README.md#evidence-labels).

**Observed** on one save, catalog [6425796](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/mynewsavenotfinished1) (temperate, 1937, 735 MB stream), against the game's line window for the line Wool1 (four trucks, Road Vehicle 255 to 258; stop 1 loads wool, stop 2 unloads). Nothing here was tried on a second game.

- **A line's id and name sit together only in `line_warning` notifications.** Each entry's `entity` is the line's entity id and `simParams.lineName` its name ([notifications.md](notifications.md#params-by-type)). The save had 14 such entries for 14 lines; Wool1 is 305,849. A vehicle's id and name sit together in the `simParams.mapping` of `cargo_rtc_warning` and `vehiclecondition` entries: Road Vehicle 255, 256 and 257 are 301,996, 303,875 and 305,653. A line or vehicle with no warning cannot be named this way. The line's id occurs nowhere else as a `u32`, `u64` or `f64` except in the records below, so the line's own record is not found, and so is not its list of stops.
- **Cargo units name their line and vehicle.** Records of five `u32` in a row, in a stretch with thousands of them: line id, vehicle id, 0, the id of the cargo's origin industry, which repeats for one line (237,445 for Wool1, Westbury Cotton Farm #1; see [statistics-lists.md](statistics-lists.md#groups)), and a clock value below the save's clock. Search for a known line id, then read the vehicle id behind it. For Wool1 this gave 25 records and four vehicles: the three named above and 303,923, which is the fourth truck by elimination (**Open**: its name was not read). Seven words (28 bytes) before the line id sits the cargo id (25, wool); the line "Wool Zachod 1" (284,733, five vehicles) also reads 25. A second shape has floats where the cargo id would be and an earlier line and vehicle before it; the line 'Wool, Wood, Gas, Meat, Plastic 1' (305,544) appears in it with Train 57 and Train 61 (ids 305,545 and 225,971 from the mapping). What a record counts (a cargo unit in transit, a trip leg) is **Open**. A vehicle with nothing aboard has none, so this gives a line's vehicles only while they carry cargo. Train 38 (line Clothes Train2, empty, no warning) could not be tied to its line this way.
- **Name slots do not give the link.** In the entity names table Wool1 sat in slot 19,065, the line Zachos Gas 1 in 19,066 and Road Vehicle 255 to 258 in 19,067 to 19,070. Clothes Train2 sat in slot 2,754 and Train 38 in 12,688. So a line and its vehicles share a run of slots only when they were made together. Over 40 vehicles named in notifications, ids did not follow slot order, and none of four slots was found next to its entity id.
- **The stop record ends with a per-cargo byte vector (Observed, 547 records).** The last field of the [stop configuration record](lines.md#the-stop-configuration-record) is `u32 37` and 37 bytes, one per entry of the save's cargo list (37 there, [cargo-ids.md](cargo-ids.md)); a byte of 1 means the stop loads that cargo. 412 records held only index 0 (passengers), 46 were empty (stops that only unload) and the rest held freight. The three stops of the line 'Wool, Wood, Gas, Meat, Plastic 1' held {25}, {7, 12, 20, 29} and {25}: wool, then meat, logs, fuel and plastic, the five cargos in its name. The records of one line follow each other. The window's 100% is the only value seen, so whether a lower percentage changes the byte is **Open**. Four records of the 60-second kind (train, tram or ship) had no vector and a shorter shape, and no 60-second record held clothes (27), so Clothes Train2's loading stop was not found (**Open**; the three 30-second ones holding 27 belong to road lines). Which entity a stop record belongs to is also **Open**: the id after the three floats of the record that matched the wool loading stop named in a cargo record (237,457) held passengers only, so that id is not simply the record's own stop.
