# Unsatisfied counts: the lists that follow spoiled fish to a stop

The `itemsAtStopUnsatisfied`, `itemsAtDestinationUnsatisfied` and `itemsAtEdgeUnsatisfied` lists of the fish chain, what they appear to count, and what `itemsLost6` did beside them. The spoiled cargo itself (the windows' counters, what a warehouse does with it) is in [spoilage.md](spoilage.md). Checked on 604 in the Small subarctic game ([fish-chain.md](fish-chain.md#a-fish-chain-read-end-to-end-windows-groups-a-truck-queue-604)).

## In short

- **A stop's unsatisfied list counts fish that arrived spoiled or unusable (Observed, one stop; "spoiled" against "refused" is Open).** It grows one fish at a time as a cart unloads, and fresh fish add nothing. The 15 spoiled fish a ship unloaded at the port left one entry of 15; the town stop later gathered 20, then 35 (the 15 again) in single-fish entries.
- **The same count sits at three levels (Observed):** the stop (`itemsAtStopUnsatisfied1_c6`), the destination group (`itemsAtDestinationUnsatisfied6`, a subset of `itemsAtDestination6`) and the route's edges (`itemsAtEdgeUnsatisfied0_c6`, `itemsAtEdgeUnsatisfied1_c6`). The edge lists place spoiled fish along the road, not tied to named segments.
- **The warehouse's counter of 15 disappeared while the town stop's count rose by 15 (Observed; the link is a fit, not a trace, Open).** Nothing was read in between to follow the fish.
- **After trucks replaced the carts, spoiled arrivals came as one burst and then stopped (Observed):** the old stock left the warehouse, and fish reaching the road stops have been fresh since.
- **`itemsLost6` is not the spoiled count (Observed).** It did not move with spoiled arrivals; it stopped when the warehouse emptied. A lead says it counts fish dropped for age before delivery, credited back to the industry (Open; the two figures differ by 35).
- **Open:** which of spoiled or refused the unsatisfied list counts, what the second stop's `itemsAtStopUnsatisfied0_c6` (47 in `1710`, owner not found) is, and whether a building refuses spoiled fish.

## Evidence and tests

### The 15 spoiled fish: a candidate group, and the warehouse keeps them

In the fish chain of [fish-chain.md](fish-chain.md#a-fish-chain-read-end-to-end-windows-groups-a-truck-queue-604), three paused saves at clock about 14.9 to 15.6 million.

- A group with `itemsAtStopUnsatisfied1_c6` (one entry, total 15, time 10,174,800) sits beside an `itemsAtStop1_c6` list (570). 10,174,800 is just before the ship unloaded its 15 spoiled fish ([above](spoilage.md#spoiled-cargo-and-itemslost-604)). It was identical in all three saves. This fits as the home of that count, matched by value and time only. **Open**: a save made after the warehouse loses them would show it change.
- **The warehouse still held the 15** in a screenshot about 4.7 million units after they arrived (2.8 times the fish delivery time of 1,687,500 units), at stock 496 of 500, while 104 fish a period were leaving it. This extends the 1.06 million units of save `1427` ([above](spoilage.md#spoiled-cargo-and-itemslost-604)): in this game the warehouse does not discard spoiled fish.

### "Unsatisfied" counts at the stops (604)

**Observed** in the same game, saves `1700` to `1710`, after the stuck cart of [fish-chain.md](fish-chain.md#releasing-the-stuck-cart-the-queue-drains-604) was released and the queue of aged carts reached the town stop.

- **An `itemsAtStopUnsatisfied1_c6` list appeared at the town stop**, in the group that holds its `itemsAtStop1_c6` list: 12 in `1701`, 20 in `1702`, absent in `1700`. It grew while the queue of aged carts reached the stop, and the carts' windows showed 11 spoiled fish in the queue before. So the list is a candidate for fish that reached a stop and were not accepted or were spoiled. The earlier stop group with 15 (the port) stayed 15. **Open**: which of the two, spoiled or refused, and whether those fish ended in the unloaded total (they did in `1702`, where arrivals and unloaded were equal).
- **The commercial buildings' lists gained about what the stop and port delivered.** Matching the 53 building groups present in both saves by the first time of their lists, the buildings' unloaded totals rose 41 and consumed 40 between `1700` and `1702`. The town stop's unloaded rose 36 and the port minus warehouse surplus rose 6, together 42. So the spoiled fish counted as unloaded and as consumed in these lists. The player suspected that spoiled fish do not add to a building's stock; the totals do not show a refusal, but a list of fish consumed is not the same as an inventory count, so the suspicion is not tested (it needs one building's window with its stock and satisfaction before and after a delivery). **Observed** once.
- **The "unsatisfied" count is a per-destination list, one entry per fish (`1701` to `1707`).** The group keyed 25,505 (the town stop's destination group) holds `itemsAtDestination6` (1,172 in `1707`) and `itemsAtDestinationUnsatisfied6`; the stop's own group holds a matching `itemsAtStopUnsatisfied1_c6`. Its totals were 9 at `1701` (12 in the stop's list), 20 from `1702` to `1705` and 35 from `1706` on. The step to 20 is 11, the 4, 4 and 3 spoiled fish of the three queued carts the player had counted. The step to 35 is 15 entries of one fish each, 10,600 to 36,400 units apart, between clock 17,427,800 and 17,636,800: one fish at a time, as a cart unloads. Fish unloaded at the stop rose by 11 in `1707` while the count stayed at 35, so a delivery is not unsatisfied by default. So the list counts fish that arrived at the destination spoiled (or otherwise not usable), a subset of `itemsAtDestination6`. **Observed**, one stop; the reading "spoiled" fits the carts' counters and the 15 below but is not proven.
- **The warehouse's counter of 15 was gone by `1707`** (screenshot at the save, clock 17,876,600, Feb 9, 1900 on the calendar): the window read fish 497 of 500 and showed no spoiled counter. The last time the player saw it (about 30 minutes of real time earlier, around `1657`) it read 15. The 15 fish therefore left the warehouse in between, and the town stop's unsatisfied count rose by exactly 15 in 15 single entries in the same stretch. That fits "the carts carried the old spoiled fish out and delivered them as unsatisfied", but nothing was read in between to follow them, so the link is a fit, not a trace. The port group's `itemsAtStopUnsatisfied1_c6` of 15 (time 10,174,800) did not change in any save through `1707`, so it is a record of the arrival, not of the count in the warehouse.
- **Fresh fish add no unsatisfied entries (`1708`, clock 18,891,800, Apr 13, 1900 on screen).** The town stop's unloaded total went 1,172 to 1,244 in the interval while its unsatisfied list stayed at 35 (last entry still 17,636,800), and the spoiled counter stayed gone from the warehouse, which read 500 of 500. The open bar of the stop's chart read 96 in the tooltip, the 13th period's change (1,244 - 1,148). The queue step was the usual 16 (1,260 loaded at the warehouse, 1,244 at the stop), and the port again counted more than the warehouse took (8 more, in a full warehouse). **Observed**, one interval; it supports reading the list as late or spoiled fish.
- **The unsatisfied counts also sit on the route's edges and on a second stop (`1701` to `1710`).** `itemsAtEdgeUnsatisfied0_c6` and `itemsAtEdgeUnsatisfied1_c6` lists appeared in `1701` (12 and 4), grew in step with the town stop's count (20 and 8 in `1702`), and in `1707` and `1710` read 15, 15, 15 and 35 in one direction and 15, 15 and 23 in the other. So the group of 15 fish counted at three points upstream, and the 35 (20 plus 15) only at the edge next to the town stop, which fits two waves of spoiled fish moving down the road, the first one already past the upstream edges. Another stop's `itemsAtStopUnsatisfied0_c6` read 17 (`1702`), 23 (`1707`), 47 (`1710`) beside a `...1_c6` of 4; its owner and why it passes the town stop's 35 were not found. **Observed**; a way to place spoiled fish along the route, not tied to named segments.

### After trucks replaced the carts (604)

**Observed** in the two autosaves of [fish-chain-trucks.md](fish-chain-trucks.md#the-same-chain-after-seven-trucks-replaced-the-carts-drained-stocks-a-second-stop-604) (clock 45,001,200 and 46,198,800).

- **Spoiled arrivals came as a burst, then stopped.** The first stop's unsatisfied list holds a carried 453 (clock 43,302,600) and then seven entries of 5, 5, 8, 12, 12, 6 and 12 between 43,591,200 and 43,849,600, which is 60 spoiled of 84 fish, the old stock leaving the warehouse. There was none at all until 44,682,400, then 9 in five entries of 1 to 3 at the first stop and 9 in five entries at the second until 44,995,800, and none in either list from there to 46,198,800. So, from the unsatisfied lists, fish reaching the road stops have been fresh for about 1.2 million units.
- **`itemsLost6` is not that spoiled count.** The three industries' `itemsLost6` lists had an entry every 100,000 to 200,000 units until clock 43,423,600, none from there to 44,916,000, and entries again from 44.9 million: 6 to 37 fish per 200,000 units, about half of what the industries load, while the unsatisfied lists stayed silent. It does not move with spoiled arrivals, and what it counts is still **Open** (this corrects a reading of mine from the first of the two saves, that its fall to zero meant fresher fish).
- **The unsatisfied lists stayed silent to clock 54.6 million, and `itemsLost6` stopped when the warehouse emptied** (autosave at 54,600,400, [fish-chain-trucks.md](fish-chain-trucks.md#the-fish-chain-at-clock-546-million-an-empty-warehouse-overflow-fish-to-buildings-604)). No unsatisfied list at the stops, the edges or destination 25,505 grew after 46.2 million. The three industries' `itemsLost6` lists have their last entries at 48.28 to 48.80 million, when the warehouse stock reached 0, and none since. From 45.0 to 48.3 million the trucks loaded 3,516 to 4,494 at the warehouse while the two stops unloaded 3,443 to 4,050: the difference grew by 371 (from 73 to 444) and has stayed at 444 to 465 since. In the same stretch `itemsLost6` rose by 406. So about as many fish left the warehouse and reached neither stop as the industries counted lost, while the old stock was being drained. That suggests `itemsLost6` counts fish dropped for age before delivery, credited back to the industry that made them. The two figures differ by 35 and the fish were not followed, so this is a lead, not a result (**Open**).
