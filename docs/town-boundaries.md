# Town boundaries and the town field

Which town owns a building, and where the dashed town boundary on the bulldozer map runs. The building record itself is in [town-buildings.md](town-buildings.md); the town's own record is in [town-records.md](town-records.md). The open questions are in [gaps/towns-and-buildings.md](gaps/towns-and-buildings.md).

- **Save `1437` (clock still 11,324,600) settles the town field: it is the owning town, and the ids follow the names table (Confirmed on 604, one game, two buildings).** The player ticked an industrial building in Retford and a house in Bromsgrove. Two flags went 0 to 1 (28 set): record 48 (stocked, level 1, 3 parcels, depth 4, 12.9 m, built 0) with town **17,912**, and record 166 (no stock, level 1, 2 parcels, depth 2, 9.5 m, built 0) with town **17,913**. With Todmorden's 17,914 from the earlier buildings, the three ids are Retford 17,912, Bromsgrove 17,913 and Todmorden 17,914, which is the order of the towns in the names table (slots 1 to 3, company id plus slot, see [entity-names.md](entity-names.md)). Then the 6 earlier ticks that carried 17,912 (three of the five houses of `1431`, two buildings of `1430` and one commercial building of `1436`) are Retford buildings by their record, and the 20 with 17,914 are Todmorden's. The player had taken all of them for Todmorden, and says a single road joins the two towns and that each town has a clear boundary on the map (it decides whose area pollution falls in, and removing something on the boundary affects both towns). So the six are not explained by streets crossing at several places; one possibility is that the street of the five houses is that connecting road, with the boundary on it (three of the five were Retford's, two Todmorden's), which the player has not checked. **Open**. The screenshots: an industrial building window with level 1, one input stock (0 of 2 of a cargo shown as a sack), workers 8 of 8, 0% supply and the box ticked; a residential building with level 1, noise Excellent and residents 1 of 1.
- **Where the Retford-Todmorden boundary is on the connecting road (player's screenshots, one game).** With the bulldozer tool active the map shows town boundaries as a heavy dashed line; it crosses the road beside the "Todmorden Maintenance Building". The three passenger vehicles the player had named (Road Vehicles 27, 22 and 23, on the line "RET-TOD Bus 1", model Landauer with 6 seats, carrying 2, 5 and 1 passengers at 20 km/h) were on the Todmorden side of that line, heading towards Todmorden. In the save the `landauer_v2` model has 20 instances; seven of them stand on that road, between about 3,600 and 4,800 m along the map's second axis (x about -860 to -1,050) and head towards Todmorden, so the three are among those seven, but the instances carry no vehicle number and which three was not told. Retford's own services (troika and `droshky` carriages) stand at about 1,430 to 1,790 on the same axis. The boundary therefore lies between those, near the maintenance building. **Observed**, one game; positions are those of a save whose clock had not moved, so the same in every save from `1428` to `1437`.

- **A fresh two-town map: the dashed line fits the line equidistant from the two towns' first crossings (604, one game, a Tiny 1 : 3 tropical new game, 2 towns, Asian names).** The player's bulldozer-view screenshot shows the heavy dashed line across the short axis between the towns. Each town's name label sits on the one node of its street cluster that has four 88 m edges, which looks like the crossing the town was founded on; in the save's frame they are at (-142.5, -1752.6) and (125.1, 1402.3). The midpoint is at y -175, 0.47 of the way along the long axis, and the perpendicular through it leans 174 m over the map's 2,048 m width. Read by eye off the screenshot the line crosses mid-height at 0.47 of the length and leans 50 pixels against 55 predicted (one pixel is about 3 m), so within the reading error it is the set of points equally far from the two crossings. Two towns cannot tell that from a rule weighted by size or by nearest building. The save holds no copy of either crossing beyond the street edge and node tables (the exact floats occur nine times each: the four edges, a node table and four records later in the stream, not identified), and no boundary-sized table was looked for. Whether the line is stored or worked out when the game loads is **Open**; tests are in [gaps/towns-and-buildings.md](gaps/towns-and-buildings.md). **Observed**, one picture.
- **Three towns, twenty years in: the lines are still the equidistant lines (604, Observed, one game, a Small 1 : 3 subarctic save of 1 April 1920, towns Retford, Bromsgrove and Todmorden).** The player's bulldozer-view screenshots show two dashed lines, one between Retford and Bromsgrove and one between Retford and Todmorden, each running from edge to edge of the map with no kink. The three first crossings were found as in the bullet above: the node with four 88 m edges of the earliest street type in each town's street cluster. Todmorden and Bromsgrove have one each; Retford has four (the town grew round them, which one it was founded on is not known), so each was tried. The circumcentre of the three towns (where the third border would meet them) is 5,000 to 6,800 m outside the map's width of 4,608 m, so no kink is expected. In the flattest screenshot (Todmorden left, Retford right) the line crosses the segment between the two name labels about 7 pixels (about 20 m) from its midpoint and leans 19 degrees from vertical against 19 degrees from the crossings' positions. In the oblique ones it runs the way the pair of towns predicts, but their perspective was not corrected. Against the first bullet's maintenance-building observation: the Retford-Todmorden border crosses the connecting road 40 to 200 m on the Retford side of the road maintenance building at x about -844 (the one the player calls the Todmorden Maintenance Building), so the building is on Todmorden's side, as are the three buses. Todmorden is far the larger town (234 town street edges against Retford's 130) and the line is still at the midpoint within the reading error, so a rule weighted by the towns' size is unlikely; a rule that follows buildings is not excluded. Whether the line is stored or worked out is still **Open**. A picture of the predicted lines on the road map is in the gitignored `local/games/XNGDTtYKhe-subarctic-small/maps/town-boundary/`.
