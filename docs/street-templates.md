# Street and track templates

What the game's own street and track templates say about a road edge's type, read from the install and not from a save. The edge records are in [road-network.md](road-network.md); this note starts from the type name an edge carries. Evidence labels are as defined in [README.md](README.md#evidence-labels).

## In short

- **The type name gives the lane count and width (Observed, 604 install, 87 templates).** The template's `laneConfigs` gives the lane count, and the save's edge record repeats that list lane by lane (Observed, 568 to 604): small types 2, medium 4, large 6 vehicle lanes, tracks 1, the tram-only street 2. The profile width is the sum of the lane widths: a small town street is 16, medium 24, large 32, a track 4 (unit taken to be metres, Open).
- **A mod's street type has no template** in the base files, so its count is not known this way.
- **Open.** The unit of the widths (no in-game measurement), and which word the game uses for `town_old_large`.

## Lane counts

**Observed**, read from the install of the 604 build (`base/content/infrastructure/street.zip` and `track.zip`, one `<type>.street_template.lua` per type, the path the save's template string names). Each has a `laneConfigs` list with one entry per lane: a direction, a width, a speed and the transport modes it takes. The lane count of a type is the number of entries whose modes include a vehicle (`CAR`, `BUS`, `TRUCK`, `TRAM`, `TRAIN` and their electric forms); the footpath lanes (`PERSON` and `CARGO` only) are left out. Over the 87 street and track templates: the small types have 2, the medium 4 and the large 6 (country and town, old and new alike); `highway_new_small`, `_medium` and `_large` have 1, 2 and 3; a track type (`simple`, `standard`, `high_speed`, with or without catenary) has 1; the tram-only street has 2; the airport runway and taxiway types have none. The save's edge record holds the same list: one entry per lane, sidewalks included, with the same speed, width, height, direction, modes and offset ([road-network.md](road-network.md#the-street-edge-table); two types compared field by field, `ind_country_old_small` and the industry siding). So the count can be read from the save as well as from the type name; `terrain_map.py` once drew widths in proportion to lanes over two (it now draws the profile widths below). A mod's own street type has no template in the base game's files, but its record's lanes give its count. The glossary's "four-lane town road" for `town_old_large` does not match the six vehicle lanes its template lists; which word is right in the game's own text was not checked.

## Profile widths

**Observed**, 604 install, the same 87 templates. Every `laneConfigs` entry also has a `width`, and the entries added up give the width of the strip a road is built on, footpaths and shoulders included (**Open**: the unit is taken to be metres, since a track is 4 and a parcel face 8 ([parcels.md](parcels.md)); no in-game measurement yet). A small town street is 16 (a 3 footpath, two 5 carriageway lanes and a 3 footpath), a medium one 24, a large one 32; the old and new town types agree, `xsmall` is 14. Country roads are 14, 22 and 30 (small, medium, large), a highway 9, 18 and 23, the tram-only street 11, every track type (`simple`, `standard`, `high_speed`, with or without catenary) 4. A lane with no mode (a highway's shoulder and median) counts in the sum. [tools/terrain_map.py](../tools/terrain_map.py) draws each edge this wide ([terrain.md](terrain-drawing.md#drawing-the-map)), by the sum of its own lanes (`width` in `street_edges.read_edges`, so a mod's type needs no install), and by `--road-width` (16 m) or `--rail-width` (4 m) when the lanes do not read.

**Observed, 568 to 601, three catalog saves: an edge's lanes can differ from today's template.** The lane widths a save stores are the type's at the time the edge was built. In 6377549 (601) 50 of 56 `town_new_small` edges sum to 14 m and 6 to 16 m, the install's value; in 6104655 (568) the 4 `entrance_old_one_way` edges sum to 9 m, against 11 in the 604 install. This fits a template changed after those edges were built (**Open**: not checked against an older install). A mod's type with no template still has lanes: `eis_os_country_tram` sums to 11 m in 3 edges of 6416180 (601).
