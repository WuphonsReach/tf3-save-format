# The cement chain read through the statistics lists

The cement chain of the [Small subarctic game](test-games.md#small-subarctic-game), from a quarry to the town buildings, read with the `items*` lists of [statistics-lists.md](statistics-lists.md) as the [fish chain](fish-chain.md) was (how to read a list's total at an earlier clock is [there](fish-chain.md#the-fish-chain-read-through-the-statistics-lists)). 604, one game, so every label is **Observed** unless marked.

## Quarry, plant, warehouse, town (604)

**Observed** in the autosave at clock 54,600,400 (6 December 1916 on screen, calendar 0.25x). Stone is cargo 24 and cement 15 in this save ([cargo-ids.md](cargo-ids.md)). Two road lines carry the chain. The first runs quarry, cement plant, warehouse: it brings stone to the plant and takes the plant's cement on to the warehouse. The second runs from the warehouse to a stop in Retford. The player's Lines window from about the same time showed the first line with 9 vehicles of two models (capacity 36 in all) and the second with 23 trucks (capacity 138). The player had not changed either line since the first months of the game.

| Point | Group (lists) | Total at the save |
|---|---|---|
| Quarry | `itemsProduced24`, `itemsDestroyed24`, `itemsLoaded24` | 5,343, 3,475, 1,872 |
| First line | `itemsTransported24`, `itemsAtStop1_c24`, `itemsTransported15`, `itemsAtStop2_c15` | 1,912 stone, 926 cement |
| Plant's stop | `itemsUnloaded24`, `itemsLoaded15` | 1,852, 926 |
| Cement plant | `itemsUnloaded24` = `itemsConsumed24`, `itemsProduced15` = `itemsLoaded15` | 1,852, 926 |
| Warehouse | `itemsUnloaded15`, `itemsLoaded15` (two groups with the same two totals) | 922, 912 |
| Second line | `itemsTransported15`, `itemsAtStop1_c15` | 912, 882 |
| Town stop | `itemsUnloaded15`; destination key 28,561 (`itemsAtDestination15`) | 882; 882 |
| Consumer buildings | 23 groups with `itemsUnloaded15` and `itemsConsumed15` | 636 |

- **The plant turns 2 stone into 1 cement and keeps neither** (1,852 consumed for 926 made, all of it loaded). That is the recipe in [industry-chains.md](industry-chains.md). The first line's stone total is 40 above the quarry's because of an early `itemsLoaded24` of 40 at the plant's stop (clock 5.6 to 8.6 million).
- **The first line carries 4 stone a trip and is the bottleneck.** Every stone arrival at the plant is an entry of 4, and the cement arrivals at the warehouse are entries of 2, the cement those 4 stone make, carried on by the same vehicle. The quarry is the Bromsgrove Quarry of [industries.md](industries.md#a-quarrys-destroyed-stone-pulse-by-pulse-and-industries-that-start-empty-604); it sits at its cap and destroyed 580 of the 880 stone it made between clock 46.2 and 54.6 million .
- **What the lists put in transit equals the Lines window's Load.** The second line: 912 loaded less 882 arrived = 30, and the window's Load read 30. The first line: 20 stone (1,872 loaded less 1,852 unloaded) plus 4 cement (926 less 922) = 24, against a Load of 23.
- **The second line's vehicles run bunched.** Its loads come in windows of 1.7 to 1.9 million units, 13 to 16 loads each, and between windows nothing loads for 1.5 to 2.6 million units. Windows begin every 3,416,200 units on average (3,454,000 in the last two cycles), and the gap has shortened every cycle (2.64 million in the first, 1.54 million in the last). In each window the first loads are 6 (a truck's capacity), taken from the stock gathered since the last visit, and the rest are 2, one per delivery from the first line. The warehouse never held more than about 26. The pattern is the line's vehicles arriving as one bunch. That 13 to 16 loads come from 23 trucks suggests the others leave with nothing (an empty departure would add no entry), which is **Open**. A trip from the warehouse to the town takes about 1.7 million units: the loads of 2 made from 51.65 to 52.34 million arrived from 53.37 to 54.08 million.
- **The edge totals show the bunch on the road.** The second line has about 140 `itemsAtEdge*_c15` groups, one run of the stream. At the save their totals step 912, 906, 900, 894, 888 and then 882 for the rest, so five trucks of 6 were on the road, and 882 is what the town stop has received. Within each run of equal totals, the last times rise toward the run's town end, and the newest of each run (54.59 to 54.60 million) is where a truck stood at the save. The long 882 run's last times fall from 54.06 million at the town end to 53.09 million at its other end, about 11,700 units per edge group (84 groups), which is the last truck before the bunch. So in this stream the groups of one direction lie in route order, town end first, and the last times trace a vehicle's passage. **Observed**, one line in one save. It is a way to tell which edge group is where, which [the fish queue](fish-chain.md#a-stuck-vehicle-the-totals-along-the-route-show-the-queue) could not.
- **The small keyed `itemsTransported15` groups are the consumer buildings' tallies.** 23 groups hold only an `itemsTransported15` list under a 5-digit key, and their 23 totals (1 to 89) equal the 23 consumer buildings' `itemsUnloaded15` totals one for one. The key is probably the building's id. That was not checked against the [town building records](town-buildings.md).
- **The buildings' lists hold 246 fewer cement than the town stop unloaded.** The difference read 52, 144, 206, 240 and 246 at clock 10, 20, 30, 40 and 46.2 million, and 246 at every later reading. It grew while the town was young and then stopped. Whether buildings that were replaced took their lists with them was not checked (**Open**).
