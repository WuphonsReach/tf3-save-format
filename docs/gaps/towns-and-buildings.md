## Town records

- **A town's position and population in the entity data, and an editor map's town names** (604) The town record has neither, and the names table of the Giant editor map held no town names. The Towns / Industries export of the map editor lists both. Home: [town-records.md](../town-records.md), [entity-names.md](../entity-names.md#town-names-604). Needs: an editor save with a typed town name, searched for in the stream.
- **The record's third and fourth floats, the floats at offsets 70 and 74, and the trailing u32** (604) The floats keep growing in play and are not the experience earned (a town with "No Experience Earned Last Year" saw the second float rise by about 5,500). The u32 of value 1 near the end is unexplained. Home: [town-records.md](../town-records.md). Needs: game window (a town's points to the next level next to the record) plus existing saves.
- **The Towns tab's per-town figures** (604) The supply figures (68 fish, 97 fuel) match neither the commercial and industrial capacities nor a simple ratio; the car-icon column tracks about half the commercial plus industrial capacities (correlation 0.96, typical error 20, outliers up to 90); the per-town demand was not rebuilt from the stocks. The 32-record catalog save 6430076 does not fit the figures once attributed to it. Home: [town-records.md](../town-records.md), [building-stocks.md](../building-stocks.md#open). Needs: game window (the tab read in a save we also hold).
- **Town windows against records and script states** (604, one game) Population, growth levels and XP-to-next were checked for the first town record only. Home: [town-states.md](../town-states.md), [town-records.md](../town-records.md). Needs: game window.
- **What `constructionBoni` holds** (604) Home: [town-states.md](../town-states.md). Needs: existing saves.
- **The Town Authority label** (604, one game) A town with an `authorityScore` of 0.859 read "None" in the window, so the label is not the score alone. Home: [town-states.md](../town-states.md). Needs: game window.
- **What the Delivery Time factor of 1.5 depends on** (604, one game) The tooltips equal the cargo files' `timeToDeliverInSeconds` times 1.5; it may depend on difficulty or `townConfig.sensitivityCargoDelivery`. Home: [delivery-times.md](../delivery-times.md). Needs: new save on another preset.
- **What the economy and climate do to town cargo slots** (604) See [cargo-and-warehouses.md](cargo-and-warehouses.md#cargo-ids).

## Roads

- **A building's position, and what the `town` value is** (604) A building record has no position, and its entity ids (the construction `d`, the parcels) did not lead to one in an editor map. A lot's ground polygon gives a place, but only a screenshot ties it to a record; in a regular game the cells a building took can be read from the parcel flags when it is bulldozed ([parcels.md](../parcels.md#deleting-a-building-604)). The record's `town` value had 18 distinct values for 18 towns and was not the same for buildings the player put in one town. Home: [town-buildings.md](../editor-map-buildings.md). Needs: new save (ticks in different towns and in one town, each with its place noted).
- **A parcel's id, and a parcel's zone type** (604, one game) A parcel's own entity id is not in the parcel table, so a building record's parcel ids cannot yet be placed on the map, and no zone (residential, commercial, industrial) is stored with a parcel. The ids are consecutive inside a normal 22-parcel street run, but the start differs per run. Home: [parcels.md](../parcels.md#parcel-ids-and-the-building-records). Needs: new save (bulldoze one building at a time on a normal run next to buildings whose records can be named, with a screenshot of each window).
- **What an all-ones `timeBuilt` means** (604, one game) 83 of the 410 records of a new game's first save have it and 327 have 0; 15 of 1,329 still have it after twenty game years, and a rebuild in place replaces it. What sets the two start values apart is not known. Home: [town-buildings.md](../town-buildings.md). Needs: existing saves (the first saves of other games and catalog maps at their start year).
- **Which of a map's town roads belong to deleted towns** (604, one editor map) The edge table keeps a deleted town's roads, a load and save does not clear them, and the free-id list holds the node ids of five of six dead towns (not of the sixth, whose freed ids a later road took, and none of a live town's). Open: where the game keeps its live entity set, whether the sixth's other ids went to other entities, whether a town enters the list at once on deletion, whether the list can replace the Towns export, and what decides which leftovers an edit clears (a new industry's access road took three). Home: [town-leftovers.md](../town-leftovers.md). Needs: new save (delete one town, save at once, read the list; save, load and save, read it again); existing saves (the other editor maps, to see if the dead clusters show the same). Tried: load and re-save (three runs), counting junction records, a coordinate scan of the stream (too noisy), a search of the whole stream for live node and edge ids as `u32`, bitmaps and byte arrays (no table found beyond the road tables). Workaround: keep only town edges near a live town from the Towns export.
- **The road tables' open points** (604, one editor map) What the lane data holds, whether the cut length depends on the street type, which leftover records an edit clears, why a deleted road's record outlasts it by one save and its strip entry stays, why 161 of 358 edges have a strip entry, and how the junction table and the free-id list are kept. Home: [road-network.md](../road-network.md#open). Needs: new save (more edits one at a time: delete a road in a town and in the country, draw a road over water, a bridge).
