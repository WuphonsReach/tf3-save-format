# Gaps: towns and buildings

Format of an entry: see [README.md](README.md).

## In short

Everything here is **Open**, seen on 604, mostly in one game.

- A building's place: its parcel ids are placed through the [entity table](../entity-table.md), so a town's buildings have positions ([town-places.md](../town-places.md)); the lot map by zone and level still needs the footprint and the Towns layer's rules ("Plotting lots" lists them).
- Town boundary: the `town` field is the owning town (Confirmed on two buildings), but whether the boundary is stored or worked out on load is open.
- Town figures: record floats, the Towns tab figures and the Authority label are not reproduced from the save.
- Stock records: which building a block belongs to is open.
- Roads: leftover records of deleted towns, the junction and road tables' remaining fields.
- Cheapest: entries marked "existing saves".

## Town records

- **The game's own town position, and a town's population** (604, 605) A town's name and its buildings' place are read through the [entity table](../entity-table.md) ([town-places.md](../town-places.md)), but the export's position, the midpoint of the town's first street, is not marked by any record found, and population is not read. Home: [town-places.md](../town-places.md#what-the-point-is-and-is-not), [town-records.md](../town-records.md). Needs: existing saves (follow the town entry's other two component types to their arrays).
- **Town places where the chain breaks** (568 to 605) No building record found in 568 and 585 (an older layout?), and a town with no building gets no point. Home: [town-places.md](../town-places.md). Needs: existing saves.
- **The record's third and fourth floats, the floats at offsets 70 and 74, and the trailing u32** (604) The floats keep growing in play and are not the experience earned (a town with "No Experience Earned Last Year" saw the second float rise by about 5,500). The u32 of value 1 near the end is unexplained. Home: [town-records.md](../town-records.md). Needs: game window (a town's points to the next level next to the record) plus existing saves.
- **The Towns tab's per-town figures** (604) The supply figures (68 fish, 97 fuel) match neither the commercial and industrial capacities nor a simple ratio; the car-icon column tracks about half the commercial plus industrial capacities (correlation 0.96, typical error 20, outliers up to 90); the per-town demand was not rebuilt from the stocks. The 32-record catalog save 6430076 does not fit the figures once attributed to it. Home: [town-records.md](../town-records.md), [building-stocks.md](../building-stocks.md#open). Needs: game window (the tab read in a save we also hold).
- **Town windows against records and script states** (604, one game) Population, growth levels and XP-to-next were checked for the first town record only. Home: [town-states.md](../town-states.md), [town-records.md](../town-records.md). Needs: game window.
- **What `constructionBoni` holds** (604) Home: [town-states.md](../town-states.md). Needs: existing saves.
- **The Town Authority label** (604, one game) A town with an `authorityScore` of 0.859 read "None" in the window, so the label is not the score alone. Home: [town-states.md](../town-states.md). Needs: game window.
- **What else moves the Delivery Time factor** (605, one game) The factor is `1.5 - (year - 1900) / 160` of the calendar year to 2019 and 0.75 from 2020 to 2032; the difficulty level and delivery sensitivity did not move it. Open: years after 2032, the other difficulty options one at a time, other calendar speeds, and 61 readings 1 s under the rule. Home: [delivery-times.md](../delivery-times.md). Needs: existing saves of later years, new save per option.
- **What the economy and climate do to town cargo slots** (604) See [cargo-and-warehouses.md](cargo-and-warehouses.md#cargo-ids).

## Town buildings

- **Where the town boundary runs** (604, one game) The `town` field is the owning town (Confirmed, two buildings); six buildings carrying a neighbouring town's id were ticked as part of another town, and whether the field is the town of the street shown is open. A two-town new game, a three-town game twenty years on and a 34-town game (pairs and three-way vertices, from screenshots) all fit the line equidistant from the towns' first crossings, which leaves stored against worked out on load open, and a rule that follows buildings not excluded. Home: [town-boundaries.md](../town-boundaries.md). Suggested tests: new save with a building deep inside a town ticked, and buildings on each side of the maintenance building where the boundary crosses a road; a screenshot of the border between two towns built up unevenly with a building standing right on it (the owning town of buildings either side of the line); the third border, between Bromsgrove and Todmorden, where the three meet beyond the map's edge, needs a map where the circumcentre is inside it; how to find a town's centre from the save (the street clusters' first crossings did not reproduce Poole's vertex on the 34-town map; the name label is the only measure that has worked); in the map editor, a town added or moved with a screenshot before saving and a byte diff of the two saves for a table the size of a boundary.
- **What fields `a`, `b` and `d` point to** (604) And whether industrial, commercial and residential buildings can be told apart (the construction entity may carry a position). Home: [building-construction-paths.md](../building-construction-paths.md). Needs: existing saves.
- **The three small tail entries** (604) `[u32 id][u32 kind][u32 n][u32 flag][u32 count][count x (i32, u32)]` changed when three buildings were ticked for Historic Preservation; which of the three ids is the home is unknown. Home: [building-construction-paths.md](../building-construction-paths.md). Suggested test: new save with one building ticked alone, to see which record the entries follow.
- **The record's `year`** (604) See [cargo-and-warehouses.md](cargo-and-warehouses.md#warehouses).
- **When a town builds on a cleared lot again** (605, once) A lot the player bulldozed was built on within about three game days; another was not while watched. Home: [parcels.md](../parcels.md#open). Needs: new save pair around one bulldozed house, a few game days apart.
- **Whether the game repairs a building that disagrees with its town** (604) On load or on a level change. Home: [building-stocks.md](../building-stocks.md#open). Needs: new save with a stock record patched.

## Plotting lots by zone and level

What a map of tinted lot footprints (residential, commercial, industrial, shaded by level) needs, in the order it is needed. A building's parcels are placed through the entity table ([town-places.md](../town-places.md)); the entries in Roads it builds on ("A building's position", "A parcel's zone type") stay the home of those questions.

- **A building's footprint in table cells** (604) Bit 0 of a flag word is set under buildings, but also on cells with trees, and a deleted building cleared more cells than its lot, on parcels of more than one street edge, so the cells that belong to one building are not the lot's cells. Home: [parcels.md](../parcels.md#deleting-a-building-604). Needs: existing saves (the delete series) and, once the ids are placed, new save (delete a building of a known lot).
- **Which level the Towns layer shades by** (604) The layer's legend has four levels per district; the record's level and the construction path's level differ for a share of buildings, and the window shows the record's. Whether the layer uses the record's level or a district level of its own is unknown. Home: [building-construction-paths.md](../building-construction-paths.md#the-zone-and-footprint-from-the-construction-path-604). Needs: game window (a Towns layer screenshot and the window of three lots of different levels in one view).
- **Whether the Towns layer tints a lot or its building** (604) The pictures show a rectangle under each building; whether it is the parcel cells, the lot of the record's parcels or the construction's footprint, and whether an empty zoned lot is tinted, is not known. Home: [parcels.md](../parcels.md). Needs: game window (the layer before and after a plot is bulldozed, at one zoom).
- **How the Towns layer derives a water industry's outline** (604) Measured for the fishing grounds and sand excavator (two boxes and three boxes of their terminal and platform models, good to about 2 m) and, for the oil platform, a box and discs standing in for tinted meshes ([models.md](../models.md#industry-footprints-the-ground-face-from-the-industrys-script)); not known is whether it uses the full or the `_sm` bounding boxes, and nothing was checked for a mod's water industry. Needs: game window (a top-down Towns-layer picture of a sand excavator and a fishing ground at about 1 pixel per 0.3 m, with the buoys in view, one per angle).
- **Zone and level for a building whose path is missing** (604) A share of the records of a played game had no construction path string whose id matched in the check that was made; the others were not looked at. Home: [building-construction-paths.md](../building-construction-paths.md). Needs: existing saves.

## Roads

- **Why a building's `town` can differ from the town the player and the bulldozer see** (604) In an editor map a house the bulldozer counted with Hemsworth carries Normanton's id ([editor-map-buildings.md](../editor-map-buildings.md)), like six buildings between Retford and Todmorden in a played game. A building's place is now read from its parcels ([town-places.md](../town-places.md)), so the two can be compared on the map. Home: [town-boundaries.md](../town-boundaries.md). Needs: existing saves (map the buildings of both towns by `town` and look at the boundary).
- **A parcel's zone type** (604, one game) No zone is stored with a parcel itself (a building's zone is in its construction path: [building-construction-paths.md](../building-construction-paths.md#the-zone-and-footprint-from-the-construction-path-604)); the parcel's row now comes from the [entity table](../entity-table.md). Home: [parcels.md](../parcels.md#parcel-ids-and-the-building-records). Needs: existing saves (an unbuilt parcel's zone).
- **What an all-ones `timeBuilt` means** (604, one game) 83 of the 410 records of a new game's first save have it and 327 have 0; 15 of 1,329 still have it after twenty game years, and a rebuild in place replaces it. What sets the two start values apart is not known. Home: [building-construction-paths.md](../building-construction-paths.md). Needs: existing saves (the first saves of other games and catalog maps at their start year).
- **Which of a map's town roads belong to deleted towns** (604, one editor map) The edge table keeps a deleted town's roads, a load and save does not clear them, and the free-id list holds the node ids of five of six dead towns (not of the sixth, whose freed ids a later road took, and none of a live town's). Open: where the game keeps its live entity set, whether the sixth's other ids went to other entities, whether a town enters the list at once on deletion, whether the list can replace the Towns export, and what decides which leftovers an edit clears (a new industry's access road took three). Home: [town-leftovers.md](../town-leftovers.md). Needs: new save (delete one town, save at once, read the list; save, load and save, read it again); existing saves (the other editor maps, to see if the dead clusters show the same). Tried: load and re-save (three runs), counting junction records, a coordinate scan of the stream (too noisy), a search of the whole stream for live node and edge ids as `u32`, bitmaps and byte arrays (no table found beyond the road tables). Workaround: `tf3map` leaves out edges with an end node in the free-id list by default (`--keep-dead` draws them); it misses a town whose ids a later road took, so the Towns export is still the check.
- **A removed industry's road still joined to the network** (605, one game) The lane test ([town-leftovers.md](../town-leftovers.md#removed-industries-the-lane-test)) catches an industry's leftover roads and sidings that hang free, not a run still joined to a live road (4 edges in one save, and a dead-end access road by a rail maintenance depot where a coal mine stood). Home: [town-leftovers.md](../town-leftovers.md#open). Needs: existing saves (look for a link from the industry's own records to its roads).
- **The lane table's open kinds** (568 to 605) Kind-2 pieces of 1 to 8 m on ids that name no node or edge, the float layout of kinds 0 and 3, whether a road's lanes hold its bend speed. Home: [lane-paths.md](../lane-paths.md#open). Needs: existing saves (pieces against station and building positions; a bent road edge's kind-3 floats against the speed layer).
- **The road tables' open points** (604, one editor map) What the edge record's first two words and the 9 bytes after its lanes hold (the lanes themselves are read, 568 to 604), whether the cut length depends on the street type, which leftover records an edit clears, why a deleted road's record outlasts it by one save and its strip entry stays, why 161 of 358 edges have a strip entry, and how the junction table and the free-id list are kept. Home: [road-network.md](../road-network.md#open). Needs: new save (more edits one at a time: delete a road in a town and in the country, draw a road over water, a bridge).
- **The junction table's open points** (604, one game) What a traffic-light phase's ids mean, whether the minimum duration works with skipping off, whether the final `u32` of the block is the Type A or C choice, where a lane-connection edit is stored, why most edge lists are empty. Home: [junctions.md](../junctions.md#open). Needs: new save (a second Type C junction; a lane connection edited at one junction; lights removed with None; a junction of 3 edges with lights).
- **The spare building record and the editor's residents** (604, one editor map) A parcel-less building record ends the run in every editor save with a town, and the next town's first building takes its id; the extra construction record and lot polygon that go with it, and why residents fall a little short of residential capacity, are unexplained. Home: [editor-towns.md](../editor-towns.md#open). Needs: new save (a town placed, then undone or a second one placed at once).
- **Whether a rebuild that lands renumbers a depot's road stub** (605) A refused road depot rebuild's proposal replaced the stub edge and its end node with new entities. Home: [construction-scripts.md](../construction-scripts.md#rebuilds), [road-edits.md](../road-edits.md#a-depot-placed-on-a-road). Needs: new save pair around one rebuild of a road depot, read with `street_edges.py --ids`.
- **Whether a road rebuilt at a depot's exit blocks its rebuild** (605) Remembered once by the player, not logged; the game rebuilt the same depot with its exit road connected several times. Home: [construction-scripts.md](../construction-scripts.md#rebuilds). Needs: new save: rebuild the road at a depot's exit, then send the depot's rebuild and read the game's log.
