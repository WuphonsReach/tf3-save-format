# Station pieces: the model instances a station is built from

A station (a port, a rail station, a bus or tram stop; the keys are in the [glossary](glossary.md)) is made of many placed pieces, and each piece is a model instance ([models.md](models.md#the-model-instance-record)). This note says which models make up the platforms and buildings and how a picture can draw them. The station's own record (modules, terminals, owner) is not read here.

## Water stations

- **The pieces are models under `stations/water/models/` (604, Observed, one game, mod.io 6425796).** In a save with five clusters of station pieces the instances were deck pieces `dock_100_25` and `dock_50_12`, `platform_100` and `platform_25`, main buildings `main_building_s0` and `main_building_s2`, railings (`safeguard_metal*`, `safeguard_wood*`, hundreds of them), clocks (`harbour_size0_clock`, `harbour_size2_clock`) and invisible path markers (`*_network_person`, `pedestrian_entrance_network_person`). The `tn_*_pier_passenger*` models are 2 m markers, one per landing a ship docks at (see below).
- **Each deck piece has thin edge pieces** with the suffixes `_con_l`, `_con_r`, `_con_b`, `_con_f` and `_end_l`, `_end_r`, `_end_b`, `_end_f` (the names suggest a continuing joint and a closed end; not tested). They fill the 2 to 3 m a plain piece leaves to its neighbour: `dock_100_25` is 97.6 m long by 22.6 m wide, and with its edge pieces fits a 100 x 25 m cell. `dock_50_12` is 48 x 9.8 m (cell 50 x 12.5), `platform_100` 100 x 50 m, `platform_25` 25 x 25 m, `main_building_s2` about 92 x 43 m.

- **Landings are the `tn_*_pier_passenger*` markers (604, Observed, one port).** The build menu offers a Small Landing and a Large Landing (`STATIONS_WATER_SMALL_PIER` is the small one; the large one's module file is `medium_pier.module.lua`, which the markers' `tn_medium_pier_passenger` names suggest), and the game marks each landing with an anchor. In the main port the save has four `tn_medium_pier_passenger` and four `tn_medium_pier_passenger_dual` markers, 2 m above the water level, and the eight anchors in the player's screenshots stand at those eight places: one on each side of each pier arm and one at each pier end. A marker's facing runs along the pier, not away from it (the markers on the two sides of an arm face opposite ways), so a picture that wants an anchor clear of the edge moves it out from the nearest deck edge and does not use the facing. The `tn_small_pier_passenger*` models (`_l`, `_r`, `_dual`, `_dual_l`, `_dual_r`) are the small landing's markers and were not checked against a picture; `tn_passenger_dock_50_12` (a marker of the 50 m dock) is not a landing and is not drawn.

## Rail and street stations

- **Rail (604, Observed, the same game).** Platforms are `rail/era_a/perron_5` (40 x 5 m, its origin at one end and the box running along +x), `perron_10` (40 x 10 m), `perron_5_underpass` (the same footprint), and the head pieces `perron_5_head`, `perron_5_head_con` (5 m, before the origin) and `station_3_perron_head`, `_side`, `_side_main`. Roofs (`perron_5_roof*`), borders (a 0.7 m strip) and `stairs` lie on or beside them. Buildings are `station_1`, `station_2`, `station_3` and their `_main` versions, in `rail/era_a/` and `rail/cargo/`, with `_con_*` and `_end_*` edge pieces; a building's box runs from its origin toward -y (`station_2` about 20 x 18 m, `station_3` 40 x 36 m). The freight terminals' add-ons (`rail_t_*`) and the `tn_underground_connection` markers (720 of them) are not drawn.
- **Street (bus and tram) stations.** The modular station's platform tiles are `street/modular_street_station/models/era_a/pltfrm_l_rep` and `pltfrm_r_rep` (10 x 10 m), with side strips `_btm`, `_top` (about 0.8 m) and `_btm_spcl`, `_top_spcl` (5 m), and `cargo_pltfrm_*` for freight stops. A side building is `sidebuildings/streetstation_sidebuilding_era_a_s2` (20 x 20 m); a small stop is `street/small_stops/small_old` (7 x 2.6 m) or `small_mid`. Centre pieces (`cntr_*`), paths (`path_*`) and entrances (`entr_*`) are roofs and stairs over the platform and are not drawn.

## The border of a bus, tram or cargo station

- **The game draws a black line round the modular station (604, Observed, one game, mod.io 6425796; shapes checked against the player's top-down screenshots of two stations).** The script that builds the station (`modular_terminal.script.lua` in the install's `stations/street.zip`) lays this polygon out as a ground face from the platform columns: each column's tile range, a margin of a few metres, and a wider stretch for the centre road column at each end. A passenger station with two platform columns of four tiles each, 30 m apart, had a 40 x 41 m platform block and a 20 m wide centre strip reaching about 7 m past the tiles at each end; the game's line followed that cross shape. A cargo station with three columns of six tiles had a stepped outline that also took in the road loop round the platforms. Whether the save stores the polygon was not searched for (**Open**).
- **The `pedestrian_era_a/*` models (5 x 5 m: `straight`, `straight_low`, `corner_in`, `corner_out`, `end_l`, `end_r`, `link_l`, `link_r`) lie along the border where it has a walkway (Observed, the same two stations).** They are not drawn.
- **A picture can rebuild the border from the placed pieces, a little under the game's size.** The outer edge of the union of the platform tiles (`pltfrm_*_rep`, `cargo_pltfrm_*_rep`) and the centre, path and entrance pieces (`cntr_*`, `path_*`, `entr_*`, `cargo_path_rep`), left without the thin edge strips, the 5 m ramp ends (`_spcl`) and the zero-size markers, leaves gaps of 1 to 5 m between pieces that show as short lines inside it; growing each box by 3 m, taking the union and shrinking it by 3 m closes them. The strips and ramp ends reach past the game's line and are not drawn as platform. The result covers the pieces and not the loop road round a cargo station's platforms, which keeps it recognisable without overstating the station.

## Sizes and drawing

- **Sizes come from the model's `boundingInfo`.** The model file (`.mdl`, text) in the install's `stations/*.zip` lists `bbMin` and `bbMax` in the model's own axes. A box (for a piece with edge pieces, the union with their boxes, its cell) placed from the instance's origin and facing (as for [depots](models.md#road-maintenance-buildings-and-depots-origin-facing-footprint-and-coverage)) matched the piers, quays, sheds, rail platforms and bus platforms in top-down screenshots of one port and its rail yard: a main quay along the shore with arms of two to four deck pieces, and three rail platforms with a station building beside the tracks. **Observed**: one port, boxes only; whether a piece's box overlaps its neighbours' beyond the edge pieces is **Open**.
- **Drawing.** `tools/terrain_map.py --stations` draws the boxes in a `stations` layer, a sublayer per kind and one of landing anchors ([tools/README.md](../tools/README.md)): water brown, rail grey, street lighter grey, platforms lighter than buildings, the street groups and the border above the roads so the border hides the ends of the station's own lanes, each group's outer edge at an industry's outline width with no line where two boxes touch (found by testing each metre of an edge for a box just outside it). The edge pieces are not drawn on their own: as separate boxes their outlines ran 1 to 2 m inside every cell.
