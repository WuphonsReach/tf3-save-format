# Subsidies

The subsidy offers, active and finished subsidies, and what they pay. A subsidy shows up in two places: as a notification entry ([notifications.md](notifications.md)) and in the state of `game_mechanics/subventions/subventions.gs`, described here. The game's own word for them is "subventions".

## The notification entry

A subsidy is a notification entry of type `subvention_notification.script`. Its `params` hold a resource path under `::/game_mechanics/subventions/` (for example `deliver_cargo/deliver_cargo.res`), `stockListEntity`, and `simParams.mapping` with the name of the industry or town. The other fields are in [notifications.md](notifications.md#entry-layout). **Observed** on 585 and 604.

## Active and completed subsidies

State of `game_mechanics/subventions/subventions.gs`, read from catalog save [6417707](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/333151) (604, start year 1900, calendar speed 1.00x, loaded paused as 17 April 2000) and compared with the game's subsidy popups. **Observed**, one save. The pair count is 8 here ([script-states.md](script-states.md#finding-a-table) for the layout).

- Top-level keys: `activeSubventions`, `completedSubventions`, `failedSubventions` (16 entries), `proposedSubventions` (empty), `lastSpawnTime`, `spawnIntervalModifier`, `usedUids` and `version` (2).
- An entry has `id` (a resource path under `::/game_mechanics/subventions/`, for example `deliver_workers/deliver_workers.res`), `uid`, `spawnTime`, `acceptedTime`, on a completed one `completedTime`, and a `data` table. In `data`: `name`, `upfront`, `complete`, `failure`, `effectDuration`, `expireDuration`, `expireDurationProposed`, and the targets (`to`, `from`, `lineEntity`, `townEntity`, `cargoType`, `toDeliver`, `delivered`, depending on the kind).
- Targets are `{entity, revision.num}` with an entity id and three numbers. Entity ids here ran up to 135,964.
- Payments match the popup to the dollar. For an active worker-transport subsidy, `upfront` 16,950,000 was "On Acceptance", `complete` 27,980,000 was "On Fulfillment", and the two `failure` amounts (16,950,000 and 31,130,000) added up to the 48,080,000 fine. In every entry here the first `failure` amount equalled `upfront`. In the other `failure` slot the money fine and a `Reputation` entry with a `townEntity` (a fraction such as 0.7) both occurred.
- Reward types seen in `complete`: `Money`, and `TownExperience` with a fraction (0.55 and 0.8) and a `townEntity`. A completed "Connect Towns" entry held 0.55 and 0.8 for its two towns, and the popup showed "55% Level Progress" and "80% Level Progress" for them.
- Times use the game clock ([calendar.md](calendar.md)). At 1.00x, with the shown date taken as 17 April 2000 (146,524,000, which is 36,631 days from 1 January 1900), the numbers fit the screens: `expireDuration` 2,556,750 is 639 days and showed as "1 Year 9 Months"; `acceptedTime` + `expireDuration` fell 93 days after the save and the progress bar read "3 Months"; `effectDuration` 365,250 is a quarter of a year and showed as "3 Months"; a completed entry's `completedTime` + `effectDuration` (a 5-year effect, 7,305,000) fell 1,633.3 days after, and the popup showed "4 Years 5 Months 19 Days", which is 1,633 days. The date was taken from the screen, so this checks the unit and the 1.00x rule against one save, not the start of the count.
- Which `data` keys each kind uses, and what `failedSubventions` holds, were not worked out. **Open**.

### The offer itself

State of `game_mechanics/subventions/subventions.gs`. **Observed** on 604, one save with a proposed industry-supply offer checked against the game's offer window.

- A proposed entry has `id` (a resource path such as `::/game_mechanics/subventions/deliver_cargo/deliver_cargo.res`), `uid`, `spawnTime`, and a `data` table. In `data`: `name` (the offer's title, "Supply Industry"), `stockListEntity` (the industry's entity id), `toDeliver` (the amount of cargo, 80 in the offer checked), `cargosToDeliver`, `delivered`, `upfront`, `failure`, `effectDuration`, `expireDuration` and `expireDurationProposed`.
- `upfront` and `failure` hold `params` tables of `amount` and `type` (`Money` in the ones seen). In the offer checked, the payment on acceptance equalled `upfront`'s 6,300,000, and the fine shown (27,000,000) equalled the two `failure` amounts added, 6,300,000 and 20,700,000. In other saves the `failure` list also held small non-money amounts (under 1), which were not decoded.
- Durations shown in the offer window equalled the stored numbers divided by 4000 (days) and then multiplied by the calendar speed, 0.50x at the time: `expireDuration` 4,626,500 showed as "1 Year 7 Months" and `effectDuration` 7,305,000 as "2 Years 6 Months". That is the rule for durations in [calendar.md](calendar.md#the-game-clock).
- The `advancedOptions.*` settings are stored as small level numbers (1 to 5 seen in the catalog saves, not multipliers): the option's position in the list plus 1, see [settings.md](settings.md#how-a-setting-is-stored). Whether a level scales the stored money amounts was not shown: at the levels in the save checked (`subventionRisk` 2, `subventionMode` 3) the shown money equalled the stored money. Across a few other 604 saves with the default levels the fine against the payment varied from offer to offer, so the fine is not a fixed multiple of the payment. **Open**.

## A new game: an offer, its expiry and an accepted subsidy (604)

**Observed** on 604, one new Small subarctic game (the series of [finances.md](finances.md#a-first-build-out-row-by-row)), three saves with the calendar stopped (date 1 January 1900, so only the game clock moves).

- **A proposal sits in `proposedSubventions`.** At clock 569,800 to 632,200 the table held one entry, a "Supply Industry" offer (`deliver_cargo`, `spawnTime` 61,000, `toDeliver` 85, one cargo id in `cargosToDeliver`, `upfront` 2,180,000, `failure` of 2,180,000 and 5,180,000). It was spawned 61 s into the game.
- **It expires on the clock, not by hand.** It was gone in a save at 1,121,000: `proposedSubventions` empty and nothing in the active, completed or failed tables. The offer's `expireDurationProposed` was 728,000, so it ran out at 61,000 + 728,000 = 789,000. A new offer was spawned at 791,600 (`lastSpawnTime`), 2,600 later. The expired offer was not moved to `failedSubventions`: that table stayed empty. **Observed**, one offer.
- **`usedUids`** keeps one entry per offer, keyed by its resource path and uid. The first offer's flag was `true` while it was open and `false` after it expired, and the second offer's (uid 35826) was `true` while active and after completion. A later save held two more uids with `true` (a `deliver_cargo_town` and a `deliver_workers` offer) that were in none of the active, completed, failed or proposed tables, so `true` is not "currently open"; what it means is **Open**.
- **Accepting books the upfront money at once.** The second offer, "Connect Towns" (`deliver_passengers`, spawned at 791,600), was accepted at clock 1,095,400 (`acceptedTime`). The journal has a `Subsidy` booking of +2,610,000 at exactly 1,095,400, equal to `upfront` (Money 2,610,000). It sits among the 39 other bookings of that tick, which are the construction and vehicle purchases the player made while paused, see [finances.md](finances.md#a-first-build-out-row-by-row).
- **What the entry holds.** `from` and `to` are the two towns, each `{entity, revision.num}` with an entity id (17,914 and 17,912 here). `complete` is two `TownExperience` rewards of 0.521 and 0.313, one per town. `failure` is Money 2,610,000 (again equal to `upfront`) and two `Reputation` amounts, 0.625 for the `from` town and 0.486 for the `to` town. `effectDuration` was 7,305,000 and `expireDuration` 1,582,750 for the accepted offer (against 4,626,500 for the first offer, a different type). If the deadline is `acceptedTime` + `expireDuration`, it falls at 2,678,150, which the player's save at 1,121,000 had not reached. Whether a failed subsidy then books -2,610,000 as a `Subsidy` booking is **Open**: wait past 2,678,150 on the clock and save.
- **The deadline is `acceptedTime` + `expireDuration`, counted on the game clock.** The "Subsidy in Progress" popup showed 22m 49s left in a screenshot taken 3 minutes of play after the save at clock 1,121,000. With the deadline at 2,678,150 that puts the clock at about 1,309,150, which is 188 s later, so the clock ran at 1.0 s per second and the popup's remaining time is deadline minus clock. **Observed**, one reading.
- **The popup, the towns and their ids.** The game's popup for it read "A subsidy to transport passengers between Todmorden and Retford without transfer is currently running", so the offer's `name` ("Connect Towns", the `deliver_passengers` type) is the title and "without transfer" is part of the rule (the player confirmed the match). `from` was entity 17,914 and `to` 17,912. In the names table the three towns sit in slots 1 to 3 (Retford, Bromsgrove, Todmorden), and an early entity's id is the company's id plus its slot ([entity-names.md](entity-names.md#layout); the company is 17,911 here), so the towns are 17,912, 17,913 and 17,914, `from` is Todmorden and `to` is Retford. **Observed**, one game; the building records confirm the three ids ([town-buildings.md](town-buildings.md#the-building-record-and-historic-preservation-604)). The same ids appear in the town states ([town-states.md](town-states.md)), which is how a subsidy's towns can be matched to a town's state.

### Completion (604)

**Observed** in a save at clock 2,246,600 (the same game, 1,150,000 units after acceptance), compared with the subsidy's window and the town windows.

- **A completed entry moves to `completedSubventions` and gains two fields:** `completedTime` (2,196,000 here) and `lineEntity`, an entity reference to the line that satisfied it (27,686, revision numbers 21, 6, 132). `activeSubventions` was empty and `failedSubventions` empty.
- **Completion books no money.** The journal had no `Subsidy` booking after the +2,610,000 at acceptance. The `complete` rewards of this subsidy were town experience only (0.521 for the `from` town, 0.313 for the `to` town), plus a timed effect, so there was nothing to book. The window's "On Fulfillment" list read "52% Level Progress in Todmorden", "31% Level Progress in Retford" and "2x Income for 2h 0m", the same numbers. The effect length `effectDuration` was 7,305,000 and the window read 2h 0m with about 50,600 units gone since completion (7,254,400 left, truncated to 2h 0m).
- **It completed without a delivery.** The player saw the subsidy complete when the first passenger was picked up and no passenger had been delivered. The save agrees: `passengerTransportedCount` in the achievements state was still 0 (and the bottom bar's passenger figure 0), against `cargoDeliveredCount` 124. Which event completes a "Connect Towns" subsidy was not tested (a first boarding, a vehicle on a line that serves both towns, or a delivery counted elsewhere). **Open**.
- **The 2x income effect was not seen in the journal.** The income bookings after the completion (2,212,000 and 2,226,400) were the same size as those before. Which vehicles the effect applies to was not read. **Open**.
- **Two towns' states rose together.** Between the earlier saves and this one the `growth_level` of the two connected towns went from 2 to 4 (Todmorden) and from 0 to 2 (Retford), while the third town stayed at 0, and Todmorden's population in its window rose from 281 to 292 ("Growing Fast"; Retford read 412, with no earlier reading). Whether the reward set the rise cannot be told from one save. **Open**.
- `spawnIntervalModifier` was 0.5 in this save (1 before) and a new offer had been spawned at 1,887,600. Whether completing a subsidy halves the interval is **Open**.

### A second offer while the first was finished (604)

**Observed** in the save 46,400 units after the completion save. A new proposal had been spawned at 2,253,000 (`lastSpawnTime`): a "Supply Industry" offer (`deliver_cargo`, uid 138,650,000) for a saw mill, 55 to deliver, `upfront` 2,850,000, `failure` of 2,850,000 and 5,400,000, `expireDurationProposed` 772,000 and `expireDuration` 8,522,500 (the first offer's was 4,626,500, so it is not a constant). `spawnIntervalModifier` had gone back from 0.5 to 1. The player dismissed its notification, and the offer stayed in `proposedSubventions` ([notifications.md](notifications.md#dismissing-a-notification-and-what-else-changes-604)). The spawn times seen so far are worked through in [the next section](#an-offer-that-expires-unaccepted-604).

### An offer that expires unaccepted (604)

**Observed** in the save at clock 3,785,800. An offer's missed-notice came at its spawn time plus its `expireDurationProposed` (plus 200): the saw mill offer spawned at 2,253,000 with 772,000 gave a `subvention_missed` notification at 3,025,200. A worker-transport offer for a quarry (notification at 2,984,600, missed notice at 3,728,000, "has become unavailable") and a "Supply Town" offer for Retford (`deliver_cargo_town`, spawned at 3,715,000, notification 3,715,800, "requires a skilled logistics firm") followed. The spawn times seen are 61,000, 791,600, 1,887,600, 2,253,000, about 2,984,000 and 3,715,000: gaps of 730,600, 1,096,000, 365,400, about 731,000 and about 731,000, so each is a whole number (1 to 3) of 365,400 within a few hundred, which makes a fixed step of that size plausible. **Open**. Missing an offer costs nothing in the journal.

### What a missed offer leaves behind, and what an autosave catches (604)

**Observed** on 604, one new game.

- **After an offer times out the amounts are gone.** The offer leaves `proposedSubventions` and the failed table stays empty, so its payments (`upfront`, `failure`, rewards) are in no subsidy table. What is left is in the notification state ([notifications.md](notifications.md)): the offer's own entry keeps its resource path, `uid`, `status` and the target entity (with the target's name in `simParams.mapping`), and the `subvention_missed` entry keeps `reason = "Timeout"`, `subventionId` (the resource path) and the target entity, with an `autoDismissDuration` of 60,000. Neither entry holds the payments, and the missed entry has no `uid`. The flag in `usedUids` also turns `false`.
- **An autosave can catch the offer in between.** The autosave made while the player was building (clock 1,095,400, `proposedSubventions` held "Connect Towns") held the full offer. Compared with the accepted entry in the save 25,600 units later, the two were identical except that the accepted one has `acceptedTime`: the payments, the `expireDuration` of 1,582,750 and the rewards are fixed when the offer is spawned, not when it is accepted. So the deadline of an accepted subsidy is its own `expireDuration` after `acceptedTime`, and `expireDurationProposed` (724,000 for this offer) only limits how long it can wait.
- **The autosaves of this series** held the game at clock 1,000, 610,600 and 1,095,400 (three in the first 85 minutes), so only two caught an offer: the first "Supply Industry" offer and "Connect Towns", both before the player acted on them. An unattended game with a steady run of autosaves would show every offer's payments, which the manual saves cannot. The game's `settings.lua` (the user's own, outside the save) holds the autosave settings as `autosaveIntervalMinutes` (the player had 15 and changed it to 5 after this note) and `autosaveKeepNumber` (20, so only the newest 20 autosaves are kept); the autosaves of this series were 54 and 16 minutes apart on the wall clock and 609,600 and 484,800 clock units apart (10 and 8 minutes of game time), so a 15-minute interval matched neither. A later test settled what the interval counts: real time, paused time included, restarting at each save ([calendar.md](calendar.md#the-game-clock)); these gaps came from a game that was not left unattended.
