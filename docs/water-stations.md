# Water stations: the pieces of a harbour in the model instances

A `harbor_modular` station (a port, [glossary](glossary.md)) is built from many placed pieces, and each piece is a model instance ([models.md](models.md#the-model-instance-record)). This note says which models and how a picture can draw them. The station's own record (modules, terminals, owner) is not read here.

- **The pieces are models under `stations/water/models/` (604, Observed, one game, mod.io 6425796).** In a save with five clusters of station pieces the instances were deck pieces `dock_100_25` and `dock_50_12`, `platform_100` and `platform_25`, main buildings `main_building_s0` and `main_building_s2`, railings (`safeguard_metal*`, `safeguard_wood*`, hundreds of them), clocks (`harbour_size0_clock`, `harbour_size2_clock`) and invisible path markers (`*_network_person`, `pedestrian_entrance_network_person`). The `tn_*_pier_passenger*` models are 2 m markers.
- **Each deck piece has thin edge pieces** with the suffixes `_con_l`, `_con_r`, `_con_b`, `_con_f` and `_end_l`, `_end_r`, `_end_b`, `_end_f` (the names suggest a continuing joint and a closed end; not tested). They fill the 2 to 3 m a plain piece leaves to its neighbour: `dock_100_25` is 97.6 m long by 22.6 m wide, and with its edge pieces fits a 100 x 25 m cell. `dock_50_12` is 48 x 9.8 m (cell 50 x 12.5), `platform_100` 100 x 50 m, `platform_25` 25 x 25 m, `main_building_s2` about 92 x 43 m.
- **Sizes come from the model's `boundingInfo`.** The model file (`.mdl`, text) in the install's `stations/water.zip` lists `bbMin` and `bbMax` in the model's own axes. A box (for a deck piece, the union with its edge pieces' boxes, its cell) placed from the instance's origin and facing (as for [depots](models.md#road-maintenance-buildings-and-depots-origin-facing-footprint-and-coverage)) matched the piers, quay and sheds in top-down screenshots of one port: a main quay running along the shore, arms of two to four deck pieces out into the water, and the main buildings on the quay. **Observed**: one port, boxes only; whether a piece's boxes overlap its neighbours' beyond the edge pieces is **Open**.
- **Drawing.** `tools/terrain_map.py --stations` draws the cells and buildings in a `stations` layer ([tools/README.md](../tools/README.md)): the decks' outer edge at an industry's outline width, with no line where two cells touch (found by testing each metre of an edge for a cell just outside it), and each building outlined with nothing inside. The edge pieces are not drawn on their own: as separate boxes their outlines ran 1 to 2 m inside every cell.
