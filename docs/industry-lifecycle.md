# Industry life cycle: creation in play, the starting count and closing

When an industry's statistics lists begin, which industries appear in play, how the Industries figure on the New Game screen compares with the save, and the deadline of an industry that is closing. The output stock and the destroyed cargo are in [industries.md](industries.md). Checked on 604, mostly in the [Small subarctic game](test-games.md#small-subarctic-game).

## In short

- **Industries made by the map have an `itemsProduced` list that starts with the marker time 18,446,744,073,709,551,615; ones created in play start at a real time (Observed, one game).** In that game the real start times were every multiple of 730,500 (half a year) minus 1, so the creation check seems to run on a half-year tick: 10 industries appeared at the first 8 boundaries. What decides whether one appears is Open.
- **Only producing industries have a list (Observed, one game).** A secondary's list is created when it is first supplied (a cement plant's list started 1,947,999, not on a half-year boundary). Closed industries were not seen to leave lists behind (the game was not played through a closure; Open).
- **The Towns figure on the New Game screen matched the save in eight games; the Industries figure did not always (Observed, eight Tiny and Small games).** The save's count is the right one (the Industries tab after loading agreed), so the screen shows the preview's own count (why: Open). The name table holds about two slots per industry, so the number of names is not the number of industries.
- **A closing industry's deadline is `closureTimeStamp` on the industry (Confirmed from the game's script, Observed in two saves).** It is the game time plus 2 years when the game decides to close, written as an `i64` in a small record that starts with the industry's entity id; the notification is stamped 600 units after the field is set and the popup counts down to the field. Candidates are industries with no delivery or shipment for 5 years. What the record's third value means is Open.

## Evidence and tests

### When an industry's lists begin, and industries that appear in play (604)

**Observed** on 604 in the same new game, reading the `itemsProduced<c>` list of every industry in the 12 manual saves and 7 autosaves of the game (the last at clock 5,997,000).

- **Two kinds of first entry.** The industries the map made have a list that starts with the marker time 18,446,744,073,709,551,615 and total 0, then the first production pulse (the seven lists of the new game, cargo ids 1, 6 (fish), 12, 18 and 24 (stone); the first pulses are at 88,200 to 120,400). The others start with a real time and total 0 and then a pulse a little later. There were 10 of those, plus the cement plant's cement list (below).
- **The creation times are half-year boundaries.** The real start times are 730,499, 1,460,999, 2,191,499, 2,921,999, 3,652,499 (three industries at once), 4,382,999, 5,113,499 and 5,843,999: every multiple of 730,500 minus 1, up to the save's clock. 730,500 is half of the 1,461,000-unit year ([finances.md](finances.md#periods)). So in this game a new industry appeared at each of the first 8 half-year boundaries (10 industries in all), and none between them. The first pulse of the fish industries followed 1 to 120 units after the boundary (1,461,000; 2,191,600; 3,652,600), so the industry exists from the boundary on. The quarry's first pulse came 112,000 units after its boundary (842,400), the first stone pulse of the stone industry created at 5,843,999 came 120,600 after it. The creation check therefore looks to run on a half-year tick, which fits the player's account that new industries appear depending on how the game is going. What decides whether one appears (the check ran 8 times with 10 industries; the chance, if any, was not seen) is **Open**.
- **West, South and East are three of them, in that order.** The fish lists created at 1,460,999, 2,191,499 and 3,652,499 are the fishing industries West, South and East. The stocks alone did not say so (West and East both read 114 fish); the production figures did (West 36, a pulse of 3 fish, and East 71, a pulse of 6), and the spawn notifications in the save settle it ([notifications-in-play.md](notifications-in-play.md#industry-spawn-notifications-604)). The three were created at 1,461,000, 2,191,500 and 3,652,500 and started with an empty stock ([industries.md](industries.md#a-quarrys-destroyed-stone-pulse-by-pulse-and-industries-that-start-empty-604)). An earlier version of this note had East and West the other way round. The other creation times belong to a `quarry` (`stone`, "North"), a `quarry` West at 5,844,000, an `oil_platform` East at 2,922,000, two `farm`, a `coal_mine` and an `iron_ore_mine` (cargo ids 1 `grain`, 18 `crude_oil`, 21 `iron_ore` and 22 `coal`, by the save's cargo list in [cargo-ids.md](cargo-ids.md#the-id-is-the-position-in-the-cargo-list)).
- **The cement plant's list is the exception.** Its `itemsProduced15` list starts at 1,947,999, one tick before 1,948,000 (16 months of 121,750), not on a half-year boundary, with its first pulse at 2,052,400. The plant is one of the map's industries. Its cement list was probably created when stone first reached it (see the next bullet). **Open**.
- **Only producing industries have a list.** The Industries tab of the overview listed 23 industries: 17 primaries (three `farm` (Crop Farm), one `forest` (Logging Camp), three `quarry`, one `coal_mine`, one `iron_ore_mine`, five `fishing_grounds`, two `oil_platform`, one `oil_well`) and 6 secondaries (one each of `cement_plant`, `saw_mill`, `food_factory` (Canning Factory), `steel_mill`, `livestock_farm` and `oil_refinery`). There were 18 `itemsProduced` lists: one per primary and one for the cement plant, the only secondary that had been supplied (the other five showed input 0 and output 0). So the 10 industries created in play are all primaries, and 7 primaries and 6 secondaries made up the map. A secondary's list is created when it is first supplied, which fits the cement plant's list starting at 1,947,999. **Observed**, one game.
- **A new game's lists first appear in the saves when they were created, no earlier.** In the manual saves made in order the first save holding each start time was the first one made after it (730,499 in `1208`, 1,460,999 and 2,191,499 in `1220-more-lines`, 2,921,999 and 3,652,499 in `1236`, the last three in `1246`). Closed industries were not seen to leave their lists behind or vanish; the game was not played through a closure. **Open**.

### The industry count on the New Game screen and in the save (604)

**Observed**, eight Tiny and Small games of ours saved at once from the New Game screen (on the first day of the start year, or the second for one, so nothing has spawned or closed in play; [test-games.md](test-games.md#map-format-tests)). The Towns figure on the screen matched the save every time (2, 2, 5, 2, 2, 2, 3, 2). The Industries figure did not always:

| Screen | Industries in the save (main-model instances, as `tools/industry_compass.py` reads them) | Name slots after the towns |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | 4 | 8 |
| 6 | 6 | 12 |
| 8 | 15 (Small 1 : 3) | 23 |
| 5 | 6 | 10 |
| 8 | 8 | 17 |
| 5 | 6 | 12 |
| 8 | 7 | 14 |
| 6 | 7 | 15 |

The two agree in three games, differ by one in four and by seven in the Small one. For the Small game the save's count is the right one: after loading it, the Industries tab listed 15 industries, the same 15 as the instance count (one Livestock Farm, two Saw Mills, one Cement Plant, two Fishing Grounds, two Logging Camps, one Oil Well, one Crop Farm, one Oil Platform, one Oil Refinery, one Clay Pit, one Brickworks and one Quarry), each with its own name and none twice. So the figure on the screen is the preview's own count and not the number of industries the save starts with. Why is **Open**; the preview may be built before the game places and flattens the industries. The Small game is also the only one with Tutorial On; whether that is the cause is the open question of [tutorial.md](tutorial.md#open-the-town-and-industry-counts).

How the save was counted: an industry is an instance of its folder's main model (`industries/<key>/<key>_sm.mdl`, or the first `_particles` model where there is none). The entity names table, after the town names, holds about two slots per industry and not one: the same name twice, and the same name again for a second industry of a kind near the same town (`<town> Logging Camp` three times for two camps). The tab shows one name per industry, so the 23 slots of that save are not 15 industries each named once, and the number of names is not the number of industries, and an industry's name can say nothing about which instance it is. The names are the defaults here: a town or industry the player renamed no longer follows `<town> <kind>`, which is the case [entity-names.md](entity-names.md#town-names-604) lists as **Open**.

### Closing: the deadline is a field of the industry (604)

**Observed** on 604 in the three saves of the saw mill's closing (`1438`, `1439` and `1440`), described with the notification entry in [notifications-in-play.md](notifications-in-play.md#industry-closing-notifications-604).

- **The deadline is a field of the industry.** The game's own script (`game_mechanics/industries/industries.script.tl` in `base/content/game_mechanics.zip`) keeps a `closureTimeStamp` on the industry. A value of 0 or less means not closing. When it decides to close an industry it sets the field to the game time plus `closureCountdownTimeSpanYears` (2, from `game_mechanics/industries/industries_config.res.lua`) times the default year length, and a command (`makeIndustrySetDespawnTimeCmd`, `api/cmd.d.tl`) writes it. It tests every 100 ticks, and only industries whose last delivery or shipment is `unusedTimeSpanYears` (5) years back are candidates. The same file also names an `extendTimeSpanYears` of 0.5, not tested here.
- **Where it is in the file.** In `1440` the value 14,622,000 appears once in the whole stream, as an `i64` in a small record that starts with the industry's entity id (a `u32`), followed by a `u32` (8,480 for the saw mill), a `u32` 1, a `u32` 0 and the `i64`. The same record in `1439` and `1438` held 0 in that place. Find it by searching for the entity id taken from the notification, then checking the shape. The records of the other industries in the same run (the `cement_plant`, `food_factory` and `steel_mill` with the third value 1; a `livestock_farm`, a `quarry`, a `farm` and a `fishing_grounds` with 5) held 0, as none was closing. What the third value means is **Open**.
- **The numbers agree.** 14,622,000 minus the countdown of 2 x 1,461,000 (a year, [finances.md](finances.md#periods)) is 11,700,000, 600 units before the notification's timestamp. The popup's 47m 11s is 2,831,000 units, which is 14,622,000 minus the save's clock. So the notification is written 600 units after the field is set, and the popup counts down to the field at the game clock.
- **A second closing (604, Observed).** The latest autosave of the Small subarctic game (clock 45,001,200) held an `industry_close` entry for a fishing industry: `timestamp` 44,460,600, `dismissed` and `expired` false, `persisting` set, and no `autoDismissDuration`. The record described above was there: searching the stream for the date 44,460,000 plus 2 x 1,461,000, that is 47,382,000, found it once, as the `i64` after the industry's entity id (49,616), a `u32` (49,454), a `u32` 8 and a `u32` 0. So the countdown rule held for a second industry, and the entry was stamped 600 after the field was set, as in the first. The `u32` after the id is not a count here (49,454); the third value was 8, where the saw mill had 1 and the fishing industries in that game 5, so it is not a type code that a fishing industry always has. What it is remains **Open**.
