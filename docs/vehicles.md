# Vehicles

Where a save keeps a vehicle and what happens to that record in play: its model instance (position and heading), how to tell which instance is which vehicle, and what a depot, a line, a purchase or a sale does to it. Checked on 604 in one game, the [Small subarctic game](test-games.md#small-subarctic-game).

## In short

All 604, one game (the Small subarctic game), so every label is **Observed** unless marked.

- **Each vehicle on the map has one model instance** (a 4 x 4 `f32` matrix: position and heading), in the same run as the animals and people ([models.md](models.md#vehicles-people-and-animals-in-one-run)). Ships, carts, buses and trucks all have one; scan every `vehicle/` model, because road vehicle models sit under both `vehicle/road/` and `vehicle/truck/`.
- **A bought vehicle has an instance at its depot's spawn point at once, before it moves (ships, carts, trucks).** Several bought together sit on one point.
- **A vehicle parked in a depot or standing without a line loses its instance after a delay** (a bus within 492,000 units of arriving, a ship between 24,600 and 148,600 units). Putting it back on its line made a new instance, in another slot and facing the depot building's own heading. A vehicle waiting at a depot on a line keeps its instance.
- **A sold vehicle's instance stays at the sale tick and is dropped within 440,800 units** (seven carts sold on the road). What happens to the vehicle in between is Open; a vehicle sold on the road may not drive back to a depot (the player's view, untested).
- **Slot order is stable between saves, which lets one track a vehicle by order.** New instances take free slots, and an instance dropped and made again lands in a different slot.
- **Depots, maintenance stations and stops have instances of their own, so they identify a slot, and a town's position can be read from its stops' instances.**
- **Open:** where a vehicle's speed, its cargo and the "go to depot" order are stored (none found), which record carries the line id found once per vehicle ([line-ids.md](line-ids.md), one Tiny game), and the lag before a dropped instance.


## Vehicle instances (604)

**Observed** on 604, one game (two saves first, 4 ships and 3 horse carts (`horsewagon_1850_v2`) on the map, then later saves of the same game). Each vehicle on the map has one model instance of the kind in [models.md](models.md#the-model-instance-record), in one run with the animals and people ([models.md](models.md#vehicles-people-and-animals-in-one-run)).

So a vehicle's position and model are in this run, though still not next to its entity id ([entity-names.md](entity-names.md#vehicles-model-name-slot-and-entity-id)). How to find a ship, read its position and heading, and what a depot does to its instance: [ship-instances.md](ship-instances.md).

## Evidence and tests
### Road vehicles: the same order between saves, and a parked bus

**Observed**, one game (the Small subarctic game, saves `1437` to `1441`, 20 `landauer_v2` buses, 3 of them tracked by the player on one bus line). Matched by order, the instances moved by distances that fit the speed in the vehicle window: at 20 km/h, 4.4 s of clock (11,324,600 to 11,329,000) gave 24.0 to 24.5 m for the buses at full speed, and shorter distances for those at stops. Two saves with the same clock (`1438` and `1439`) held identical instances. One bus (Road Vehicle 27) was ordered to a depot; the save with it standing in the depot had it at the depot's own position, 12 m from the depot building's model instance (`depots/road/road_depot/road_depot.mdl`, the depot's translation read the same way, with a second `road_depot_sm` instance at the same point). Before the order it had stood 28 m from a road maintenance station (`depots/road/road_maint_station/road_maint_station.mdl`), which is where the player saw it. So a depot and a maintenance station are placed things with their own instances and can be used to tell which slot is which vehicle. The bus kept its instance at the depot in that save (clock 11,983,800), but not in the next one, 299,200 units later with no change made in between (clock 12,283,000): 19 `landauer_v2` instances instead of 20, none within 60 m of the depot point, and the other 18 in the same order as before. So a road vehicle drops its instance after parking, as [the ship](ship-instances.md#ships-in-a-depot-bought-parked-and-added) did (between 24,600 and 148,600 units after parking); the bus was still driving at clock 11,791,000 and parked by 11,983,800, so it kept the instance for less than 492,000 units after arriving, a bound not narrowed further. **Assigned to the line again** (save `1443`, same clock 12,283,000 as the save before it, paused, one change): the instance was back, 20 `landauer_v2` again, at 126.6 and 4,949.4, 24 m from the depot's own point and 12 m from where the bus had parked, facing the opposite way (0.64, -0.77 against the parked -0.64, 0.77, which is the depot building's own heading). It stands at another place in the run, slot 10 instead of 19, so the order of the run is not kept when an instance is dropped and made again, and the other 19 instances were identical to the earlier save. **Tracking it afterwards by order** (saves `1443`, `1444`, `1445`, the count staying at 20): the 20 instances kept one order, every step fitted the speed (the longest, 3,511 m in 932,400 units, against 5.2 km at 20 km/h), and slot 10 read as the bus at each save: at the depot (127, 4,949), then 391 m away at the first stops of the line in the depot's town (-190, 5,180; the player said it sat in traffic behind trucks there), then at (-1,150, 1,910), 3,408 m away (3.66 m/s, 13 km/h, which is the speed the window showed) and 6 m from a `small_old` stop in the line's other town, where the player saw it just leaving the stop with six passengers. The stops are `stations/street/small_stops/small_old.mdl` instances (17 in that game), and no instance of a stop sign model existed. In that game the depot's town (the player's "Todmorden") lies at the end of the connecting road with the larger x and y (its north-west end) (stops at x -170 to 120, y 4,980 to 5,260) and the other town ("Retford") at the other end (south-east) (stops at x -1,265 to -1,150, y 1,450 to 1,920), so a town's position can be read from the stop instances of its lines. Its six passenger records read `(0, 1)`, from the first stop to the depot's town ([trip-plans.md](trip-plans.md#trip-plans-line-stops-and-vehicle-or-a-building-604)). The order itself (the "go to depot" mark) was not found: all 192 vehicle instances were identical before and after it.

### Road vehicles bought: an instance at the depot at once (604)

**Observed**, one game (the Small subarctic game; seven saves). The road vehicle models sit under both `vehicle/road/` and `vehicle/truck/` in the table (`horse_cart_small` is `vehicle/truck/horse_cart_small/horse_cart_small.mdl`), so scan every `vehicle/` model, not one folder.

- All seven vehicles of one line were sold: the next save had seven instances fewer (three `horsewagon_1850_v2` gone, four `horsewagon_1850_usa_v2` gone) and nothing else changed.
- Five Small Horse Carriages (`horse_cart_small`) were then bought and the game saved paused at once. The save held five new instances (4 became 9 of that model), **all at one point**, 25 m from the road depot's own instance, with the game paused. So a bought road vehicle has its model instance at the moment of purchase, like the ships bought and not yet sent ([ship-instances.md](ship-instances.md#ships-in-a-depot-bought-parked-and-added)).
- After about a second of game time at 1x, one of the five had moved 2 m and four were still at the spawn point. In the save with all five on the road, they sat 25 to 150 m from the spawn point along the road, and two saves later (the first one at its first stop, loading) they were 5 to 60 m further on, still short of the line's first stop. The name slots and the header count of the new vehicles are there at purchase too ([entity-names.md](entity-names.md), [header.md](header.md#counts-the-load-dialog-shows)).
- Where the cargo of a vehicle is stored (the first `horse_cart_small` had 1 of 4 loaded in the last save) was not looked for.
- **Seven trucks bought in one go (saves `1958` and `1959`, same clock).** The model `benz1912_box` (a 1912 truck, id 4345 in that save's table) had no instance before and seven after, within 0.6 m of one point at the Todmorden road depot (x 125.2 to 125.8, y 4,950.4 to 4,951.1), so a bought road vehicle is placed at its depot at once like the ships and carts above. The seven sit 5,904 to 238,371 bytes apart in the stream (free slots taken as in the ship tests). In the same save the seven horse carts that were sold left no visible trace: counts of every `vehicle/` model were equal except the seven new trucks (`horse_cart_small` 11 and 11, `horsewagon_1850_usa_v2` 5 and 5). The player says the carts were sold while out on their line route, not in a depot, so they had instances, and a sold vehicle's instance stayed in the stream after the sale at the same clock tick. **An autosave 440,800 clock units later** (clock 43,802,000, 18 August 1912, play speed 4) had `horse_cart_small` at 4 instead of 11, the seven trucks still at 7, the road vehicle count still 83, and `horsewagon_1850_usa_v2` (5) and every other vehicle model unchanged apart from cars and people, which come and go. So the sold carts were `horse_cart_small`, and their instances were dropped some time in those 440,800 units (the header count had dropped at the sale). When within that span was not narrowed, and what happens to a vehicle between the sale and the drop was not watched: the player thinks a vehicle sold on the road does not drive back to a depot, but has not tracked one after such a sale (so the parked bus above, which lost its instance 299,200 units after reaching a depot, may not be a comparable case). **Observed**, one game; the lag and the vehicle's fate are **Open**.
