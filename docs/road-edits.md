# Road edits: what building, splitting and deleting a road did to the tables

What one change per save did to the street-edge table and its neighbours: a stub deleted, roads of several types built, a long road cut into equal edges, a curved road, a town deleted and its leftovers cleared. The layout of the table is in [road-network.md](road-network.md). All of it is **Observed** on 604, in one map editor game ([the Giant temperate editor game](test-games.md#giant-temperate-editor-game), eleven saves, `0643` to `0654`).

## In short

- **A deleted road's record stays in the street-edge table for at least one save, and is removed with a later edit (Observed, also across a reload).** The junction table and the ground polygons show a deletion at once; the edge table does not. Counting edges, buildings or towns in a save can include ones the game no longer has.
- **Deleting a town removed none of its edge, building or strip records (Observed, once):** only the per-town counters, the junction records at the country road's end and some ground polygons changed. A later edit cleared three, then six, then two of its eleven leftover edges; whether the cleanup follows the edit's place or only the number of edits is Open.
- **A long road is cut into the fewest equal pieces that are 100 m or shorter, `ceil(length / 100)`, and a bend is a node (Observed, seven roads, four street types).** A curved road is several edges, each bent along its tangents ([edge-curves.md](edge-curves.md)), whose tangents match at the shared node, so the curve is smooth across nodes.
- **A new road of n edges takes n + 1 node ids and n edge ids from the free-id list at the end of the stream,** plus a few extras; freed ids can reach the list only when the game loads and saves again.
- **Open:** what the new junction record ids after a town deletion are, which leftovers a later edit clears, and the order of the free-id list.

## Evidence and tests

### What the edits did

One change per save, on the same stretch of the main road of the town Hemsworth. The coordinates are map metres.

| Save | Player's change | Street-edge table |
|---|---|---|
| `0643` | Baseline | The stub below, node `2a78f` at (-3423.8, 12371.1) to dead end `85be` at (-3467.8, 12294.9), 88 m, is one record |
| `0644` | Three buildings marked Historic Preservation | Unchanged. Three flag bytes went 0 to 1 in town building records ([town-buildings.md](town-buildings.md)) and nothing else in them changed |
| `0645` | Deleted the stub, a road segment ending in a dead end off a T-junction | **Unchanged**: the stub's record is still there, as is its dead-end node |
| `0646` | A `country_old_small` road from the main road, 58 m | The stub's record and the T-junction node are gone. The two 88 m edges beside the junction became three edges of 55, 55 and 66 m, with a new node `85bf` at (-3442.9, 12382.0). The country road is `85bf` to a dead end `7e56` at (-3414.4, 12432.3), 57.9 m |
| `0647` | A standalone country road with noise barriers, about 118 m | Two new edges of 59.4 m: `7de1` at (-3466.5, 12442.2), `85be` (-3431.8, 12490.4), `85dc` (-3397.0, 12538.7); 118.9 m in all. The game cut the road in two at its middle |
| `0648` | Loaded `0647` and saved at once | Unchanged, all 361 edges (see below) |
| `0649`, `0650`, `0651` | A house ticked; the town deleted; a load and re-save | Unchanged, all 361 edges, in all three ([the deletion](#deleting-the-town)) |
| `0652` | A chevron of Large Country Road With Noise Barrier drawn over the old town's ground: 148 m, then 98 m at 90 degrees | Three new edges (see below); three of the town's leftover edges removed |
| `0653` | A medium-width country road with noise barriers in three segments: 100 m, 101 m at 115 degrees, 249 m at 45 degrees | Six new edges; six more of the town's leftover edges removed |
| `0654` | One curved four-lane town road along the coast (type `town_old_large`) | Four new edges of 75 to 77 m that turn together by 72 degrees; the last two town leftovers removed |

- **A long road is cut into equal edges, and a bend is a node.** The 118.9 m road became two edges of 59.4 m and the 57.9 m road one edge. In `0652` the 148 m segment (147.6 m) became two edges of 73.8 m and the 98 m segment one edge of 97.8 m, joined at a node where the road turns 90 degrees (a corner is not a curved edge here: both edges are straight). `0653` tested the limit with segments the player labelled 100 m, 101 m and 249 m (the game's length readout): 99.8 m stayed one edge, 101.2 m became two of 50.6 m, and 249.3 m three of 83.1 m. All seven roads seen fit one rule: a segment is cut into the fewest equal pieces that are 100 m or shorter, so `ceil(length / 100)` pieces. The game's Angle readout (90, 115 and 45 degrees) equals the angle between the two edges' directions in the save, to within a degree. The type of the new edges was `country_old_large_barriers` (and `country_old_medium_barriers` in `0653`), and the node ids ran `2a23d`, `2a252` (the midpoint), `2a257` (the corner) and `2a28f`, in the order the player drew them.
- **A curved road is several edges (Observed, 604, once, `0654`).** A town road drawn as one curve along the coast, of the type the player called a four-lane town road (`town_old_large`, base type `town_old`, the same base as `town_old_small`), became four edges whose chords are 76.6, 74.9, 74.9 and 76.6 m (302.9 m in all), between five new nodes (`2a479`, `2a4a1`, `2a4a7`, `2a4c0`, `2a4db`); the two end nodes have one edge each, so the road stands alone. The direction of the tangent turns by 18.3, 17.9, 18.0 and 18.3 degrees per edge (150.7 to 78.2 degrees, 72.5 in all). The tangent at a shared node is **the same** in both edges (132.4 degrees at `2a4a1`, 114.5 at `2a4a7`, 96.5 at `2a4c0`), so the curve is smooth across the nodes, and a tangent's length (75.7 to 78.7 m) is close to the edge's length, where a straight edge's tangent is exactly its chord. A circle through each piece's ends gives about 307 m for the whole curve, so four pieces of 100 m or less fit the cut rule above, now for a curve and a fourth street type. The game's length readout was not read, so this is a fit and not a measurement. The four lane-data blocks look the same as on the straight edges (the float 16.67 at the same place).
- **A deletion is not in the table at once.** The record outlasted the deletion by one save and was removed in the next, which also held another edit on the same road. On another map it outlasted a no-edit save and went with the next new road ([editor-saves-tests.md](editor-saves-tests.md#a-delete-reaches-the-edge-table-with-the-next-new-road)).
- **What a town's deletion preview covers (screenshot of `0649`, not saved).** With the bulldozer on the town marker the editor highlighted Hemsworth's roads between the junctions, but not the junction squares themselves and not the three country road edges, and said the town's four historic buildings would be removed. The road east of the new junction (`85bf` to `797f`) and the road west of it were highlighted. What the deletion then did is the next section.
- **The crane flock** `#278` of [animals.md](animals.md) (9 birds, flying) stood at (-3520, 12291) in every save compared, 55 m from the stub's dead end, so a flock is a fixed landmark in an editor map and tells which part of a screenshot a coordinate is.

### Deleting the town

**Observed, 604, once** (`0650` saved right after deleting Hemsworth with the bulldozer on its marker, `0651` a load and re-save of it). Both previews show the town's ground empty, with only the two country roads left, so the game no longer has the town. In the stream:

- **What changed.** The per-town counters of the statistics state (`townResidentialCount`, `townCommercialCount`, `townIndustrialCount`) went from 19 groups each to 18. A `happiness_residential` list appeared 53 times, as many as the "(53)" on the town's marker (probably its residents; a guess). In the junction table the country road's three-edge junction record (edges `0x1b50b`, `0x746b`, `0x731f`) is gone and records with new, higher ids (about `0x2d561` to `0x2d722`) were added: one dead-end record and two three-edge ones. So the country road's end node did not just become a dead end in that table, and what the new ids are is **Open**. About 70 ground-polygon points were removed and 147 added around the old junction, and one flying bird's z changed by 0.08 m.
- **What stayed, in both saves.** All 361 street-edge records, including the town's own roads, the 335 strip entries, the 18 town records and the 1,104 building records with the 4 Historic flags set. The load and re-save changed none of this either. So a save can keep the records of a deleted town: counting towns, buildings or edges in a save can include ones the game no longer has. A later edit cleared three of the edges, not the rest (next section).
- **Against the predictions made before the save.** The country edges stayed with unchanged positions (right). The edge table kept the town's roads and the building and town records stayed (wrong: expected one of them to change, and the stale-record rule of `0645` held across a reload too). The junction did change at once but not into a plain dead end. The animal list changed in one entry.

#### A later edit clears some of the leftovers

**Observed, 604, `0652` to `0654`.** The chevron was drawn right over where the deleted town's main road had run (its middle node is 6 m from the old node `8693`). Against `0651`: the three new edges came in, and three of the town's 88 m edges were removed from the street-edge table, while the other eight of the eleven town edges in that area stayed (one of them 2 m from the new road, so it is not about overlap). The three were at the old town's far end, 61 to 223 m from the new road, and two of them end in a dead end; other dead-end edges of the area stayed. The next edit (`0653`, a road drawn nearby) removed six more, leaving two of the eleven: `7cd7>8693` and `8693>7a0e`, the two that meet at the node `8693`, which the chevron's first segment passes within 6 m. `0654` (a curved road 79 m from the end of `7a0e`, no overlap with either) removed the last two, so by then all eleven were gone, three, six and two in the three saves that followed the chevron. Every edit since `0652` cleared some; whether the cleanup follows the edit's place or only the number of edits is **Open** (the chevron drawn over the old road in `0652` did not clear these two; a road 79 m away did). After `0653` the 18 town records, 1,104 building records and 335 strip entries were still the same, and the flying bird `#319` above the new road dropped to z 41.85 (42.07 before the deletion), as the ground under it was regraded. The 18 town records, the 1,104 building records, the 335 strip entries and the animal list were the same as in `0651`.
